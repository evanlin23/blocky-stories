# Asset licences

Every file under `assets/` is listed here, one row each. All of it is CC0 1.0 (public domain): free for commercial use, no
attribution required (credited anyway). Nothing NonCommercial, nothing from BBC Sound Effects. Each licence was checked on
the asset's own page when it was downloaded on 2026-11-03. Owner, 2026-11-02: "yes go ahead with sfx and visuals"
(RULES.md, hard rule 6). The score stays synthesized; characters, faces and stories stay ours.

Audio was converted to mono 48 kHz 16-bit WAV, trimmed, and (steps and ambience) low-passed until under 5% of the energy sits
above 4 kHz; ambience was cut to a steady stretch and crossfaded into a seamless loop. Textures are the 1k colour and roughness JPGs (grass
re-encoded smaller); HDRIs are the 1k .hdr files.

Total size: 34.6 MB.

Added 2026-11-03 from the owner's own browser download (PR #102): Kenney Impact Sounds, RPG Audio, City Kit (Suburban) and
Nature Kit, and Quaternius Ultimate House Interior, Ultimate Nature and Ultimate Buildings. Each pack's License.txt is copied
into `assets/licenses/` (Quaternius ships none, so its file quotes the CC0 line from the pack's page). Kept: a curated subset
only, with no characters, creatures, lettering or signs (the Buildings pack's `*_Sign_*` models and the Furniture Kit were left
out).

Licence pages: OpenGameArt shows each entry's licence on its page; Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/license (all assets
