Every file under `assets/` is listed here, one row each. All of it is CC0 1.0 (public domain): free for commercial use, no
attribution required (credited anyway). Nothing NonCommercial, nothing from BBC Sound Effects. Each licence was checked on
the asset's own page when it was downloaded on 2026-11-03. Owner, 2026-11-02: "yes go ahead with sfx and visuals"
(RULES.md, hard rule 6). The score stays synthesized; characters, faces and stories stay ours.
