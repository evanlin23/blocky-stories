# ep16-lookout: brief, redo 2 (writer, 2026-11-02)

This replaces the 2026-10-31 brief (the forest giant that steers by the river), which is retired. Old builds are in git history.

**The script is the brief.** `script.md` has everything a builder needs: the big idea, the rule, the plants, the cast, the hourglass marker, the shot plan beat by beat, and at the top, under "BUILDER: what changes and what's reused", the full list of set and `story.json` changes.

## In one look
- **Rule (line 1):** "In this valley, time runs slower the higher up you are."
- **Marker:** a small hourglass a hand above every head, pale-gold sand falling fast low down and slow up high.
- **Big idea:** four nights in the highest lookout are twenty years for my brother below. The tall stranger who climbs my stairs is him, older than me, who spent his life moving higher to slow his own sand.
- **Loved one:** Ollie, my brother, a teen at the gate (L08, about 0:14), an adult from L31.
- **No creature.** The scare is a man on the radio using my nickname, a light crossing the valley, the gate clanking, steps on the stairs and a tall man in the door lit from below.
- **Length:** about 1:27 to 1:31, 46 lines, 380 words, about 65 shots.
- **Look:** keep the current (non-closer) style for the tower, and the montage town can be bright. `look.flash_safe: true`.

## Build order
1. Test still first: S47 (the tall man in the door, lit from below, his hourglass over him) and S50 (the reveal face, same face design as teen Ollie). Iterate until the man reads as a threat at phone size and the reveal reads as Ollie in one look.
2. The hourglass marker on every character, and the L01 two-shot (it's the cover, `cover_s` 1.2).
3. The montage town, the night town lights per night, the pines' per-night scale, the hut.
4. Retire the giant and its traces from every shot.

## Standards risks
- Teen Ollie appears only in daylight at the gate, never in peril. The man in the door never touches me.
- No text, numbers or real places in the set. The hourglasses have no numerals. The badge is our own glyph.
- Every light is steady or fades over 1 s or more. Ollie's window never blinks.
