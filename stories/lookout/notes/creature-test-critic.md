# ep14-lookout: creature test still, critic (round 1)

**Verdict: revise.** Scary 4/10, silly 4/10. The lights work, but the giant isn't in the picture.

1. **The first half-second.** A hollow stump with glowing eyes beside a fire lookout: "an owl in a tree hole", or "a haunted stump with lit windows". The lit yellow-green boulder reads as a mossy hill with a pine, and the stump's pale, cracked right face as a silo. There's no figure, because the log chest, shoulders and arms are all black fog.

2. **Scary 4/10:** two warm points in a dark split at arm's length is right, and they're the brightest pixels. But the camera floats outside the rail, so we watch a diorama. The hollow glows red-brown like a lit room, and two even, centred orbs in an egg make a cartoon face. **Silly 4/10:** not a toy, but it leans cute (a forest spirit peeking out), with bark like crumpled foil.

3. **Scale and the narrator.** Neither reads. The head is cab-sized, so it's a big stump by a hut, not a 23 m person. No forest pine is in frame, and the ground 20 m down never shows. The hood is a dark cube and the plaid a blue stripe, half behind the corner post and overlapping the hollow. At 270 px I'm a stack of crates.

4. **The same world?** The set, captions and grade match ep11 to ep13, but the creature doesn't. Its sculpted bark and round boulder look like a nature asset, and no R6 block body shows. Note 18 asks for "a figure built like everyone else but wrong", and there's no figure here. The cold rim also turns the stump pale grey, ep11's colour.

5. **The three changes, in priority order:**
   1. **Put the camera on the deck, for fear.** Behind my right shoulder at 1.2 m, tilted up 10°. My hood and `terrified` profile take the lower-left third, lamp-lit, a tan face against the black hollow. The hollow fills half the width, the lights in its upper third, with the lip half-hiding the near light so it peers instead of reading as an owl. Use it for L42 to L44.
   2. **Make it a silhouette, not a lit object.** Kill the lamp key on the boulder and the pale rim on the stump. The head and both stones become one dark mass, thinly rimmed against lifted fog: hump, stump, hump, the R6 shoulder line. Take the hollow to near-black, warm only near the lights. Remove the black sliver in the fog (x 790 to 950, y 1110 to 1140).
   3. **Give scale its own still.** The scare's fog top hides the body, so one still can't do both. The film needs this catwalk close for fear, plus a full-body wide before the scare: 03's daytime fog top, with the log chest, both stone humps and the stump above the canopy, and the tower as the ruler. This 3/4 can be L45's cutaway after fix 2.

## Pass 2

**Gate: revise, for framing only.** The creature design passes, so freeze it. It's no longer silly or cute, it's block-world, and the narrator reads as an R6 character. Both fixes are camera and pose changes that the set build doesn't depend on. The brief's road low wide test still is still owed.

**Fear still (L42 to L44): scary 6/10, silly 3/10.** It reads as a scared blocky face with one glowing eye in the black behind it. The half-hidden light peers, the face sells the distance, and it's the same world as ep11 to ep13. Two things hold it back:
- My eyes sit at y 0.83 to 0.88, under the Shorts title and channel row (checked with a mock overlay), and the frame edge cuts off the left third of my head. The best thing in the frame is covered.
- The head fills the width with no sides showing, so its jagged rim reads as a mountain ridge.

**Scale still (03, L30): scale 7/10, scary 3/10, silly 2/10.** It's clearly tower-tall, with the pines at its chest and the sapling as a good ruler. Seen from straight behind, the arms are flush with the torso, so it reads as a slab with a chimney: a ruin or rock stack as often as a figure. The lower-left foreground pine overlaps its arm, which blurs the edge. The square timbers and faceted stones match the world.

**The two fixes:**
1. **Tilt the fear camera further down, not up:** about 20° instead of 10°, and pan about 5° left.
   - A down-tilt raises everything in frame. By my projection for the 15 mm lens, the eyes land near y 0.76 (under the caption, above the feed UI), the lights near y 0.40, and the rim at the top edge, which ends the mountain read.
   - The pan brings my whole head into frame and keeps the lights left of x 0.75, clear of the button strip.
   - Re-check under a feed mock.
2. **Make the scale wide read as a body.**
   - Swing the camera about 35° round its back to a rear three-quarter, keeping the tower in frame.
   - Pose it mid-stride, R6-style, so the far arm swings forward as its own diagonal block, with bright fog between the near arm and the torso.
   - Delete the lower-left pine, and keep the tower inside x 0.85.
