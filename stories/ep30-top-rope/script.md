## Premise, rule, twist

- **Premise:** I climb late on Thursdays, alone on the auto-belay, in a climbing wall built inside an old brick mill. Since a slip last year (nobody hurt) I won't let anyone hold my rope. Each Thursday, when I look up from the top hold, someone is standing upside down on the overhang ceiling above the route, one hold nearer the edge. Sam, a quiet regular, asks every week to belay me, and every week I say no.
- **The rule:** my own. Nobody holds my rope (L03, L14).
- **Twist:** the auto-belay box has been broken for weeks. A climb is built for two, one on the wall and one on the other end. The Headstander has been my other end every Thursday: my rope runs past the broken box, over the anchor and up to the ceiling, where it's tied round its waist, and it leans back on the ceiling like a belayer on a floor. "Something HELD me" turns from a thing grabbing me in the dark into the belay doing its job. It's the same moment on both watches. The first line is the scare (L58 to L61), heard before we know what held.
- **Where it's planted:**
  - **Plant 1, the red tag.** A plain red tag (no text) swings from the auto-belay box. Seen in S08, named at L31 to L32, and Sam looks straight at it at L38. It's in her hand at L70.
  - **Plant 2, the rope.** The lower is too slow for a machine (L28), and the rope comes down cold and dusted in lilac, though our gym chalk is white (L29 to L30). S29 shows the lilac dust on the coiled rope next to a white chalk bag.
  - **Plant 3 (bonus), the creak.** "Like a ROPE / going TIGHT." (L44). The black harness round its middle (L23) is where the rope is tied, in shadow until L66.
- **The last 15 seconds:** Sam holds the rope, I let go on purpose, she catches me and lowers me, and the rope is warm for once. Then L89 repeats L01 word for word and L90 says "Now it's SAM." On a rewatch, L01 to L03 play as the belay, and "NOBODY / was holding / my ROPE" is the line I believed, and wrong.
- **Canon call:** a Keeper never touches anyone. Nothing touches my ankle or any part of me. The pitch's "Something HELD / my ANKLE." became "Something HELD me / at the TOP / of the wall.", where the holding is the rope going tight on my harness. The Headstander's hands are never on me or near me, and it never grips anything on screen (the rope is tied round its waist, so it needs no hands).

## World

An indoor climbing wall built inside a tall old brick mill. Soot-dark brick, cast-iron columns, heavy timber beams, tall arched windows that hold a deep evening blue. Iron factory lamps on chains hang from the beams (warm tungsten). The wall is grey-green plywood panels with muted holds (ochre, slate, brick red, cream), 12 m high. Near the top it leans out into a 3 m overhang roof covered in holds, which is the ceiling the Headstander stands on. At the top of the route there's a steel anchor ring on a small steel plate, and beside it a grey auto-belay box with a red tag. Blue crash mats along the base. Floodlights at the foot of the wall point up the panels (the creature's key from below). A bench, a chalk bucket, a coil of rope. No text anywhere: no signs, no grades, no brand on the box or the tag.
