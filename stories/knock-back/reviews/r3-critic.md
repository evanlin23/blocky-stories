**Overall: 8/10, approve.** The patch delivered what I asked for. S69 went past it, S59, S63 and S57 are clean, S68 got fixed along the way, and the R7 first frames are clean. The designer's two weak spots are real but small: S66's knuckles read as "its hand's end", and S01's board reads as a strip of light. Neither is worth reopening the last round. Ship it.
