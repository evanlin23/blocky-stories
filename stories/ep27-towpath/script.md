## The story

- **Premise:** I live on a long boat on a quiet canal with my old dog, Moss. For twelve years, at first light, I slid the hatch and said "Good morning, Moss", and she climbed the three steps, jumped onto the towpath, and rolled in the wet grass by the back mooring ring, face up, eyes on the sky. Then we walked the towpath at her pace. This summer her legs got slow, and she can't make the steps or the jump. I still say good morning. She wags from her bed, and I step round her and walk the towpath alone, fast. This autumn, at dawn in the fog, something lies down on the towpath: a person built as long as my boat, lying face up, one mooring ring nearer each dawn.
- **The rule:** the boat's own, and the first line says it: the day starts with good morning, then something lies down on the towpath. For twelve years that something was Moss. It's never called a rule. It's shown as the old morning in L03 to L06.
- **The twist:** the Long Man was never coming for the boat or for me. It was coming to Moss's spot, the worn patch of grass by the back ring, which has been empty every dawn since summer. A boat is counted with everyone aboard, a dog included, and the morning roll in the grass was hers. When she couldn't get there, something lay down in it for her, the way she always did: face up, eyes on the sky. The first line's "something lies down on the towpath" first reads as the creature. The end shows it was always Moss, and the last line says her name in its place.
- **Where the twist is planted** (two plants a rewatch catches):
  - **Plant 1 (L05, about 0:10):** "face UP, / eyes on the SKY." It's said of Moss in the warm past, by the back ring. The same words are said of the Long Man at L24 and L53. A rewatch hears the echo, and sees the back ring in both framings.
  - **Plant 2 (L11, about 0:24):** "her worn PATCH / by the back ring / had grown OVER." A shot of the grass under my bunk window, grown in. The Long Man's head ends up exactly there (L46). At L36 its eyes are already on that grass.
  - **Also for the rewatch (picture only):** shot 1. Behind me at the hatch, the long dark line lies far up the towpath, and the near grass by the back ring is empty. In the last shot, Moss lies in that grass.
- **The last 15 seconds:** L68 to L72. I carry her off every dawn, we walk at her pace, and the last line says the first line again with "something" turned into "MOSS". The thing the first line was scared of is a dog in the grass.
- **World:** a narrowboat on a still, nameless canal in early autumn. Navy hull with cream coach lines, a short back deck with a sliding wooden hatch over three steps down into the back cabin, my bunk with one small round window on the towpath side, Moss's bed at the foot of the steps. Beside it, in the past shots, a worn patch of earth the size of a dog. In the present it's grown in with grass. Dawn fog on flat water, willows on the far bank. No bridges, locks, signs, boat names or any text anywhere.

## The creature: the Long Man
