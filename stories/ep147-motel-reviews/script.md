## The big idea
**At this motel every review card comes true, and the polite man in our closet was never the motel's: he was the last guest, kept in Room Six by a card that said he never left, and when I write him out, the card he leaves behind is about my dad and me.**

- **Wait, what:** the closet man isn't a haunting. He's what happens to a guest someone reviewed. The joke review (the bucket pool) was the same power, aimed at a room. Aimed at a person, it keeps him.
- Ten pitched, this one kept. Dropped: the clerk is a creature (ep87's failed still), the motel eats guests (graphic), the man is a ghost (death read), the cards are written by the motel (unexplained power), we write our way out cleanly (no sting), Dad wrote the card (confusing), the man is my future self (crowded), the cards rewrite the past (time), the closet leads somewhere (a door to elsewhere, owner's example family), the man is a mannequin (rule 1: dummy).
- Checked against "Claimed big ideas": ep87 was "words make the place" (the clerk and the bad rooms written into being). This one is "words keep a person", and the twist is the next card. Not ep96 (a card with a double reading) or ep121 (a fortune given). Nobody is hurt or taken.
- **The rule is the clue:** "you get whatever the last guest wrote" is said as a joke in L02. It's the whole mechanism.

The narrator finds an old card saying that man was a guest who never left, writes a new card that checks him out, and he leaves, but he leaves a card saying the narrator and his dad never left, and now his dad has started folding shirts the same way.
