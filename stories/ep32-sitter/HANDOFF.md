with both faces clear at phone size, bigger pine patches with a dark outline, and the lore strokes. The builder kept
fighting tight sets (easels and heads crowding the frame), so start by blocking every two-shot from a still sheet
at 300 px, before rendering. Queue note from standards: at least 7 uploads after mirror-maze, never next to
floor-thirteen, and not the same week as ep88-six-paintings.
