## The big idea
**If you swim alone at our pool, someone always swims with you, and the someone is whoever swam your lane last: the splash one lap behind me is me, from yesterday, and tomorrow I'll be the one behind.**

- **Wait, what:** Grandpa's cheerful "lane buddy" is Grandpa. The pool keeps every swim and sends the last one back to keep you company.

At this town pool anyone swimming alone always gets company, and when the narrator swims alone something unseen splashes one lap behind him and even copies his odd double kick. He works out that the pool sends back whoever swam that lane last, which was himself yesterday, so tomorrow he'll be the one swimming behind.
