Verdict: revise

Checked on the quarter-res preview and the stills. Fiction, kids and mature themes, and IP pass: there's no lettering in the set, the cast are all adults, the Half's flat side reads as built and not as hurt, and every face is our own.

1. **Originality (hard rule 5, ep20).** This is ep20's spine again: estranged grown siblings, a Keeper in the gap one of them left, the gap closed, and a split object made whole ("Ours is SHARED" where ep20 shares a torn strip). ep20's warmth is on the never-again list. Keep the coin rule and the Half. Change the loved one or the warmth, drop the estrangement (L12, L13, L26), and give L59 to L66 an ending that isn't two halves joined.

4. **Package.** The description says "thirty nights", but L14 says "Twenty". "A month ago" fits thirty, so change L14 to "Thirty nights in a ROW."
5. **Package, `cover_s`.** Add `"cover_s": 6.4` (S04: the moon coin lit over a worried face, under "really DOESN'T.").
