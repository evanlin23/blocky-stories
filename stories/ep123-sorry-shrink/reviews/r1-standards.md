# ep123-sorry-shrink: standards, round 1 (script stage)

Verdict: revise

Fiction, kids, IP, flash (no light change), title, pin and `cost` opening pass.

## Must-fix
1. **L10, L11 (the batch-1 list, a "Not X, it was Y" cadence):** "She's not POLITE. / Every inch / was a GIN." Change L10 to a plain statement, for example "She's a card SHARK." Keep L11.
2. **L05, L12 and the description (the count doesn't add up):** losing 400 in a row, with a sorry on every win, is 400 inches, and "fifty years of beating EVERYBODY" is more again. She's lost about 60 inches (5 ft 5 down to 5 in). Make the numbers agree: L05 "I've lost / SIXTY games / in a row.", L12 "Sixty Sundays / of beating ME,", and "four hundred" becomes "sixty" in the description.
3. **S06 (the meter contradicts itself):** the tag drops on three Sundays in the montage but still reads 5 at L07. Leave the tag at 5 and put a small down-arrow glyph on each circled Sunday on the calendar instead.
4. **S07 and the continuity note (constraint 2, a held object near a face):** a playing card is nearly as tall as a 5-inch aunt, so pushing one "at chest height" puts it at her face. Show her winning cards already lying flat at her feet, and have her nudge the last one into the fan with her shoe, hands at her sides. S11: "each card longer than she is tall", not "taller".
