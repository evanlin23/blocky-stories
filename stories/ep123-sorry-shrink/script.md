## The big idea
**In a town where every "sorry" shrinks you an inch, my mug-sized aunt isn't the politest woman alive: she's the town's unbeaten gin player, and every inch went on the "sorry" she says each time she lays down a winning hand.**

- **Why it clicks:** she only shrinks on Sundays (L06), I've lost four hundred in a row (L05), and the one sorry we see is said looking me in the eye over her cards (L07, L08).
- Ten pitched, this one kept. Dropped: she says sorry for me (ep47 and ep49's "someone carries mine"), she shrinks to fit a dollhouse (a creature-ish prop), she's hiding from someone (ep112), she's sorry to her plants (no turn), sorry is a curse she passes on (ep119's trap), she grows back by thank-yous (new magic), she's never said sorry and someone else shrinks her (muddled), the town shrinks her on purpose (mean), she shrinks to win hide-and-seek (ep112), she's mug-sized because she's always apologizing for Grandpa (sad).
- Checked against "Claimed big ideas": not ep116 (praise makes you grow, pride waiting on something): the price runs the other way, and nobody waits on anything. Shape `misread`.
- **The rule is the clue:** "Every sorry shrinks you" sounds like a tax on kindness. After L11 it's a scoreboard.
