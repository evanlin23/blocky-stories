## The big idea
**In the quiet car, anyone who says a word rides on out of sight until somebody answers them, so the "empty" car I've ridden in silence for six years is full: forty people who each said one word and never got an answer, my cousin among them, all leaning in every morning, hoping the quiet guy will talk.**

## The story
