## The big idea
**In our town your phone can't run out while you're waiting for someone to call, but it never charges past one percent, and my cousin Dex's has said 1% for three years, waiting on a call from someone he won't name, and it's me, and my own phone has been at 1% for the same three years because I've been waiting on him.**

- **Why it clicks:** Dex's phone is stuck at 1% (L03), so someone he loves hasn't called. My own phone shows 1% in four shots before I notice (S01b, S05, S11, S13), and I think it's just old (L18). The bet over who'd call first was three years ago (L10, L11), the same three years. When I call, both phones are free to run out at last (L15, L16).
