## The big idea
**In our town your phone can't run out while you're waiting for someone to call, but it never charges past one percent, and my cousin Ray's has said 1% for three years, waiting on a call from someone he won't name, and it's me, and my own phone has been at 1% for the same three years because I've been waiting on him.**

- **Why it clicks:** Ray's phone is stuck at 1% (L03), so someone he loves hasn't called. My own phone shows 1% in four shots before I notice (S01b, S06, S12, S19), and I think it's just old (L27). The fight was three years ago (L09, L10), the same three years. When I call, both phones are free to run out at last (L23, L24).
- **Ten pitched, this one kept.** Dropped: Ray's waiting on a stranger (no warmth), on his ex (romance), the phone is waiting for a call from the future (that's ep139's series), Grandma's phone is the twist (smaller), the battery drops when you lie (ep154's lane), the phone drains when someone forgets you (the crowded forgotten theme), the phone rings by itself (eerie only), I fix the phone (no rule), Ray changes his number (no turn), we both call at once and get busy signals forever (a trap).
- **Checked against "Claimed big ideas":** not ep128 (a family-distance meter kept at zero on purpose), not ep107 (Dad buying my bad days), not ep118 (a faked feud). A battery as a meter of waiting, and both people waiting. Shape `both-waiting`.
- **The rule is the clue:** "while you're waiting" sounds like it's only about Ray. The other phone in the story was waiting too.
