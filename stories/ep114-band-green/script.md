## The big idea
**The band on the green plays for the saddest person in town and won't stop until they dance, and all summer the whole town has been dancing every Saturday, past three in the morning, because the saddest one was me, on my bench, since my best friend moved away.**

- **Engine:** the dance that won't stop. No folk tale's shoes or film's band.
- **Why it clicks:** the band showed up the week after Benny left (L14), whichever way they spin every dancer ends each song facing my bench (L18), and the rule says "everyone" and I'm part of everyone (L35, L36).
- Ten pitched, this one kept. Dropped: the band are Keepers counting dancers (a count), the band is the town's dead musicians (death words), the band plays until someone is taken (peril), the dancers are puppets (horror with no warmth), I'm the band in disguise (ep86's shape), the band plays for the happiest one (no stakes), the band is my grandpa's old band reformed (a sad family reveal), Benny is in the band (a silhouette twist), the music is in my head (muddled), the band plays forever and we live with it (no climax).
- Checked against "Claimed big ideas" and every twist: not ep54's dance hall (no glyph halves, no partner's spot), not a stand-in, not the forgotten theme (I'm seen by everyone). Shape `for-me`.
- **The rule is the clue:** "everyone has to dance. Everyone." first reads as a curse with one lucky exception. After L31 the exception is the point: it's all for the one who won't.

## The story
- **Premise:** Every Saturday night this summer a band nobody knows has played on the town green, and the moment it plays, everyone dances. Everyone but me: I sit on the bench Benny and I used to sit on, and my feet never move. Last Saturday the band didn't stop.
- **Rule / hook (L01, about 2.7 s):** "On our town GREEN, / everyone has to DANCE / to the band. / EVERYONE." L02: "Except ME. / I've never danced / ONCE."
- **The relatable want or fight:** missing a best friend who moved away, and not feeling like joining in.
- **The marker (the rule meter), on screen by frame 1 and in every shot:** a small cream tag top-left with a music-note glyph and a song counter (1 to 47), and a tiny row of footprint glyphs that light when people dance. Mine, the last one, stays dark until L37.
- **The 3 to 5 example montage (L06 to L10, 9 to 19 s):** the bank manager doing the robot, the mail carrier spinning on her bag, Grandpa's knees clicking on the beat, a man dancing while he finishes a sandwich.
- **A concrete mystery on screen by 15 s:** nobody knows the band, nobody's seen their faces (L04, L05), and I'm the only one sitting (L02).
- **How the narrator is the exception:** the only person in town the music never moves.
- **The loved one at stake:** my best friend Benny, named "My best friend, Benny," at L13 in a photo on my phone of the two of us on this bench. Grandpa, named at L09, is the face of the tired town.
- **First real scare (L19 to L27, about 32 to 48 s):** song forty, three in the morning, the town drooping and still dancing, the bandleader tipping his brim up to dark and two points of light, and every face on the green turning to my bench.
- **The climax turns the rule's own logic:** everyone has to dance, and I'm part of everyone (L35, L36). I stand up and dance, badly, and the song ends on the next bar. No new magic.
- **Twist and reveal (L28 to L34, about 48 to 59 s):** said plainly (L29 to L31) over one picture (S27: the whole green of dancers, every face turned to my bench, still dancing, the band rim-lit behind them, and me small on the bench in the middle of it).
- **How the last 15 seconds turn over the first line:** the band packs up, the town lies on the grass laughing, I call Benny, and next Saturday it plays for someone else, and I'm the first one on the grass, which runs straight into L01.
- **The plants a rewatch catches:**
  1. L18: every dancer ends each song facing my bench.
  2. L14: the band first came the very next week after Benny left.
- **Ending shape:** the exception turns out to be the reason, and joins in.
- **Warmth:** getting up for the people my sadness was costing (the town, and Grandpa's knees), then calling my best friend. The rule's cost, never a town being quietly kind (r1).
- **Sad family reveal?** No.

## Clarity test (cold, two sentences)
In this town, when a strange band plays on the green everyone has to dance, except the narrator, who's never danced since his best friend moved away. The band plays for the saddest person in town and won't stop until they dance, so the whole town, his grandpa's sore knees included, has had to keep dancing all summer because he wouldn't, and when he finally stands up and dances, the music stops and he calls his friend.

## World

## The creature (the band: people made wrong, never fully shown)
Creature-light: five players, never fully shown, never the whole body lit at once. `docs/research/creature-playbook.md` section 2, applied while writing:
- **Wrong silhouette (rule 1):** five musicians too tall for the bandstand, their heads bent forward under its roof, hat brims pulled down to their chins, arms a little too long for their horns. At a glance, a band in white jackets, then wrong. Not a robot, mascot, snowman, appliance, box, dummy or costume, because the horns and the stooped heads read as people first.
- **What's stretched (rule 2):** necks and torsos, about 1.5 times the cast's, so they stoop. Arms long, hands at the end of white sleeves near their knees when not playing. Built from `kit.box` in `set.py`, one model posed five ways.
- **Scale proof (rules 3 and 13):** S04 and S25: the bandstand's roof edge pressing their hat crowns, a normal-height dancer's head at their chest at the bandstand steps.
- **The one lit identity cue (rule 12):** the white jacket shoulders and the brass horn bells, caught by the bulbs on the bandstand eaves behind them.
- **Light (rule 5):** from behind and above, the eave bulbs giving a hard rim to hats and shoulders, and a little from below off the brass. Faces in full shadow under the brims. No warm front light on them, ever.
- **Eyes and mouth (rules 7, 8, 15):** none until L26: under the leader's brim, darkness and two tiny `kit.emit` points of light. No mouth.
- **Materials (rule 9):** off-white jackets with a gray cast, black trousers, near-black hats.
- **Where they are in each beat:** L01 out of frame (sound). L04 the band at the bandstand, framed from behind the crowd, rim-lit. L05 a close on hat brims. L06 to L10 behind the dancers, soft. L17, L18 the bandstand at the back of frame. L20 to L24 rim-lit silhouettes, horns up. L25, L26 the bandleader's brim tipping up an inch. L27 behind the turned crowd. L40 lowering their horns. L41 walking off into the dark beyond the string lights, seen from behind, too tall and slow. Never seen moving fast, never off the bandstand until L41, never touching anyone.
