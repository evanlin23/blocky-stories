## The big idea
**My aunt the locksmith has a key to every house on our court and locks them all at midnight, and the thing she's racing round the court every night is me, sleepwalking home since I moved out, so she locks every door but hers to make sure the only one I can open leads to my old room.**

- **Why it clicks:** ten locks click every midnight and mine never does (L13, L14), my sneakers are wet every morning (L15), she skips her own door (L26 to L29), and she waits on her porch with a blanket (L30).
- Retried from ep86 with its claimed twist ("the intruder was me"). The new parts: one porch instead of the whole court on camera, warm-lit faces, no glowing tags, and a new warm turn. I stop sleepwalking because I start walking over awake, for dinner, and she keeps my room anyway.
