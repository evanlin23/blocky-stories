## The big idea
**The storage block's motion lights have never once seen any of us. They only see the tall grey one, and every "click" that brings the lights back, the one everyone thinks their waving causes, is it taking a step. A dark that lasts means it's standing next to you, and it only takes what moves.**

- Checked against "Claimed big ideas" and every twist. It isn't forgetting, a stand-in, a reflection, time, a swap, a disguise or a loop. The closest is ep81's "a count wrong on purpose", but nobody lies here. The marker everyone trusts measures something else entirely.
- **The rule is the clue:** "The motion lights go off if nobody moves for a minute" is true word for word. "Nobody" just never meant us. "You wave, and CLICK, they're back" (L04) is also true, just not because of the wave.

## The mechanics (airtight, said plainly in L30 to L32)
1. The sensor sees only the tall one. People moving never count, and never have.
2. It can only move in the dark. Lit, it's frozen. So after every lit minute the sensor times out and the lights go off.
3. In the dark it takes one step, to the next door. The step trips the sensor, the lights click on, and it's frozen again before anyone sees. So it's always one door nearer, and nobody sees it walk. That's a **short dark** (about 1 s).
4. **The superstition:** people always wave in a short dark, and the click comes a second later, so everyone believes waving brings the lights back. It's a coincidence every single time (L04, L05, L13). L16 and L17 break it: we don't wave, and the click comes anyway.
5. **It only takes what moves** (L32). If it's already next to someone when the lights go off, it doesn't step. It waits, and the dark stays. That's a **long dark**. If they wave (everyone does), it takes them, then steps into their spot, and the click brings the lights back. Gone, never shown hurt.
6. If nothing beside it moves, it goes to whatever moves nearest. In the L33 to L35 dark that's the balloon at eight, one door past us. That step clicks the lights on.
