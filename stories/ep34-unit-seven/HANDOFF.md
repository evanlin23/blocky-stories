# ep34-unit-seven: handoff (2026-11-03, revival try)

**Status: dropped again.** No PR.

## What was done
- New big idea, "Hold Still" (engine: the rule read a second way): the storage block's motion lights only ever see the tall one. Each click is it taking a step in the dark. A long dark means it's standing beside you, waiting for you to wave, and it only takes what moves. Five pitches were tested on cold readers and this one read right. The full script also passed a cold-viewer test (7/10) and had one fix round. `script.md`, `package.json` and the `stories/ideas.md` claim are the new story.
- `set.py`: `build_deep` was rewritten three times for a new tall grey creature. `story.json` and `generator/` are still the old story's and were never rebuilt.

## Why it dropped
The creature failed the critic's phone-size test still on all three tries:
1. 4/10: a crash-test dummy, with a chest slab that read as a lunchbox, an upright lit face and short arms.
2. 4/10: a wooden posing mannequin, with rod limbs, joint gaps, an egg head and stick hands.
3. 3/10: a grey block robot or golem with a visor-like brow. The face was lit with bold eyes, the arms ended at the hips, and it didn't read as taller than the cast at depth. It's also near the batch-1 ban on "a pale grey blank-faced creature".

## If someone revives it
The story idea held up. The creature is the blocker. Next time, design the creature before anything else, with a real silhouette concept (not a grey humanoid) that is shadow-faced and bowed, in a non-grey material, and staged close to camera so its height reads. Test it in the L26 framing.
