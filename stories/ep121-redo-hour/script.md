## The big idea
**Everyone here gets one ten-second redo an hour, and everyone's told nobody else remembers, but a redo has never rewound anything: it only snaps you back to where you stood ten seconds ago, and the whole town has always watched it happen and politely acted like the first take never did, including the old barista who's watched me do it nine thousand times.**

- **Engine:** the world remembers your resets, because it watches them. No film's loop.
- **Why it clicks:** every redo in the town is a social one (a proposal, a trip, a wrong word), Vic keeps wiping the counter every time I redo (L23), and he says my first-take order after a redo (L14, L15).
