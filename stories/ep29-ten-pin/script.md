## Premise, rule, twist

- **Premise:** I'm new at the warehouse, and my workmates Dev, Rosie, Mags and Tomasz drag me to their Thursday bowling league at a tired old alley. From my first frame they call me "the Spare", and I hear it as the spare part, the one tagging along. Their score screen has six rows: four of them, me at the bottom, and a fifth row with no picture that scores on its own. At the far end of a lane, in the dark behind the pins, stands a bowler in our teal team shirt with a second head stacked on its own, the top head's eyes shut. It's one lane nearer ours each week, and the top eyes open a little more each time.
- **The rule:** "When the fifth row lights up, nobody bowls." (Dev, L14.) Everyone stands back and the fifth row scores by itself.
- **The rule broken:** week four, the fifth row lights up, and Mags looks at me and says, "Your go, Spare." I step up in the fifth row's turn (L45 to L47).
- **The scare:** the sweep lifts on a fresh rack and it's standing among the pins in our own lane, both heads looking straight down the boards at me, top eyes wide. The lights dip, and when they come back it's halfway up the lane (L49 to L55).
- **The twist:** the team was five until their friend Juno moved away in spring. Every Thursday since, the fifth row bowled itself, and they called whatever bowled it "the Spare". They named me after the thing keeping Juno's place, hoping I'd take it, and Mags's "Your go, Spare" was them handing it to me. When I bowl in the fifth row's turn, my picture slides up into the fifth row and the thing is gone.
- **How the last 15 seconds turn the first line over:** "They call me the Spare" opens as a small insult (the extra, tagging along). At L76 it's the name of the team's fifth place, given to me on purpose: a welcome. "I'm the FIFTH" pays it off and loops straight into "They call me / the SPARE."
