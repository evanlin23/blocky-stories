## The rule and the twist

- **The rule (never stated until the twist):** the lift only goes to 13 when nobody in it is talking, and two people riding in silence count as alone.
- **The plants** (both by about 25 s, both re-read at the twist):
  1. **The balcony wall (about 12 s).** Mr. Okafor next door has never said hello. But most evenings, over the low balcony wall, I hear him talking to his tomatoes. He talks, just never to people.
  2. **The phone ride (about 25 s).** Wednesday I'm on the phone the whole way down, arguing with my landlord about the heating, and the lift doesn't stop once. It plays as relief, not a clue: no hold and no sting.

## Button count: 3x3, nine buttons, nine turns

- **Decision: a 3x3 grid, so nine buttons and exactly nine spoken turns that darken them.** A 2x2 head is four big round lights in a square, and at head size that reads as a game pad, a toy phone or a four-eyed robot. Kid appeal is the one risk this channel can't carry. Nine reads as a real lift keypad, which is the wrongness we want. It gives the lit "eye" room to travel, and nine all lit at once is a wall of cold light. Nine turns also give the friendship room to happen on screen. Four turns ("talk to me / about what / anything / tomatoes") is a transaction.
- **Cost:** about +35 words over B's four turns. Turns are short (2 to 6 words plus a tag), so the total stays about 610 to 630 with all three solo trips. Trim trip 3 only if it passes 640.
- **The test still confirms or overturns it.** The critic views both at phone size. **3x3 stands** if the grid reads as lift buttons (not a keypad toy), one lit button reads as an eye in the doorway frame, and all nine lit is frightening rather than a scoreboard. **Overturn to 2x2 only** if the 3x3's buttons blur into one patch at phone size after the designer has tried larger buttons on the same 0.54 m panel, **and** the 2x2 doesn't read as a toy. If it flips, the talk goes to four turns (B's own) and the saved words go back into trip 3. The designer tells the scriptwriter and me the same day, before the script is locked.
- `set.py` currently builds 4x4 (16). That isn't an option: 16 turns won't fit. Set `BUTTONS = 9`, `GRID = 3`, the eye is the centre (index 4), and `LIGHT_ORDER` is rewritten for 3x3.
