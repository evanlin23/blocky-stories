# ep22-model-village: standards review, round 2

Verdict: approve

Checked on the quarter-res preview (137.1 s, -14.2 LUFS, -1.7 dBTP), the contact sheet, the stills sheet, a mid-frame of every shot, `preflight` and `check`.

1. Fiction: pass. L33 now says "OWN cottage" and L57 "BIG tea shop" in voice and captions. The word "real" is gone. The set still has no text or signs, and no real places are named.
2. Kids and mature themes: pass. The whole cast is `age` "adult". Nobody is hurt and there are no death words. "The monster was ME" (L55) is fine.
3. IP: pass. The faces, hair and outfits are our own. No game or toy name appears in the package or on screen.
4. Originality: pass. `check` is clean. The setting, the thumb-sized creature, the first line (a sight) and the warmth (community) don't repeat anything in batch 1 or this batch. The lore plant (the seamless stone under the green, S17) is one background detail, and the story doesn't need it.
5. Flashing: pass. The preview reads 1 flash and 3 changes a second at most, no red, and no extended run. The final is measured on Actions. Two stretches carry a low risk. The first is S50 to S53b (94.6 to 99.6 s), where full-frame warm glass cuts against dark. The second is the bright highlight on S67 (128.8 to 131.0 s) cutting to S68. Keep both fades soft.
6. Package: pass. The pin, "Would you have kept sanding?", asks about L17, early in the story. The description now matches the title ("one window further up the hill") and says "It's made up." It uses no banned words, dashes or semicolons, and `made_for_kids` is false.

Nice-to-have

- S67 still has a clipped white blob at the left edge, and it's the brightest spot near the end.
