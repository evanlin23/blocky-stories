# ep126-nine-oh-nine: standards, round 1 (script stage)

Verdict: revise

Fiction, kids (the 17-year-old is only narrated), IP, pin and description pass.

## Must-fix
1. **The title, against ep98-town-whistle (hard rule 5, queued):** "The Whole TOWN Hides At 9:09" has the same shape as ep98's "Our Town Hides When The WHISTLE Blows", and both rules send the whole town inside. Retitle away from "town hides", for example "Be Home By 9:09 Or You're LOCKED Out 🕘". Add a queue note that ep126 and ep98 are at least 7 apart.
2. **S21 and its cover (constraint 3, nothing that has to read as a shape):** two dancers as outlines against the moon must read as "two people dancing" at phone size. Replace it with one face: from the ladder top, Grandma's face over Grandpa's shoulder, in moon shadow with her features readable, the red lipstick catching the lamp glow, still not lit enough to name. `cover_s` moves to S33.
3. **S22 to S24, S38, S42, S43 (constraint 2, no raised hands near a face):** hands on shoulders sit beside both faces. Stage the dance with hands at waists or joined at waist height, nothing above the shoulder line.
4. **S12a, S12b (constraint 1, at most two light changes):** several shades sliding over lit windows are light changes on top of S05's fade. Have every shade already down at S12a, with one last one sliding slowly (1.5 s) at S12b, which is the second and last change.
5. **L01 (the recipe, at most 12 words and 2.2 s):** it's 13 words and about 2.6 s. For example: "Out past nine oh-NINE here, / nobody lets you IN." L02: "Not till dawn. / Nobody knows WHY." Keep `cost`.
