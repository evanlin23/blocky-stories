## The big idea
**Everyone in town has been indoors by 9:09 every night for sixty years and nobody knows why, because the rule was made up by my grandma at seventeen so the streets would be empty for her first date with Grandpa on the feed store roof, and they still meet up there every night.**

- **Why it clicks:** Grandma's always asleep by nine (L25), Grandpa's knee is always too bad for stairs (L26), the feed store ladder's rungs are worn smooth (L19), and the curfew has no reason anyone can name (L02).
- Ten pitched, this one kept. Dropped: something walks the streets at 9:09 (a creature), the town is a set (ep94's staged world), the mayor made it up (a tired template), a Keeper counts the doors (stand-in), the rule protects a sleeping giant (night-mower, killed), it's a loop (ep115), the curfew keeps people from seeing the stars (soft), the roof figures are ghosts (death words), it's for a surprise (ep94), Grandpa made it up (less fun: Grandma's father is the reason).
- Checked against "Claimed big ideas": not ep103 (a street's kind cover story for Grandpa: here one teenage girl made up a rule for herself, and the town just followed it), not ep113 or ep85 (no wonder run by hand). Shape `no-author`.
- **The rule is the clue:** "Nobody knows why" sounds like the town's dark secret. After L32 it's a girl's trick that worked too well.
