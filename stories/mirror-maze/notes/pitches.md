# ep14-mirror-maze: three premises (scriptwriter, 2026-10-31)

This is story 2 of 4 in batch 2, row 2 (`mirror-maze`) of `reels/blocky/batch2-pitches.md`, for PandaNanoAdventures (note 21). The working title is "Only one of us walked out of the mirror maze". It's a made-up creepy blocky story, never claimed as true, with no lesson. It runs about 2:15 to 2:30, which is roughly 560 to 640 words at voice A's 290 wpm, and never over 3:00. Every line is `who: narrator`. Tasha and the maze guy only speak in reported speech.

## What all three keep from row 2

- **Setting:** the last weekend of a travelling fair, in the rain, half packed up. Rides sit under tarps, and rails and panels are stacked on truck beds. The mirror maze is the only ride still lit. Its bulbs burn steady and never chase.
- **Cast:** me and Tasha, best friends since forever, both `age` "adult". The only other person is the maze guy, an adult in a rain poncho who's taking the maze apart. He knows nothing and says one plain thing about time at most. There are no other fairgoers, and nobody young anywhere in frame.
- **The creature, the copy:** Tasha's own rig in her yellow raincoat, with the same blocks, skin and hair.
  - Its eyes are mirror-black with no highlight. That's `faces.py`'s "hollow" eyes with no glow, next to Tasha's own eyes, which have the shine.
  - It copies me the way a mirror does. It faces me when I face it, and it does what I do half a beat later.
  - It moves on screen, in the light, all the time. That's the opposite of batch 1, where every creature was never seen moving.
  - It's never pale grey, never thin, never rods or tentacles. It never touches anyone, and it's never explained.
- **First line type:** an inside joke. Whoever says PICKLE first buys the fries. Each pitch sharpens it a different way, and in each one the joke proves which Tasha is real.
- **The warmth:** loyalty. The fair is packing up and everyone's leaving, and I go back into the maze for her. She's "still in there", never taken, hurt or in danger, and she comes out soaked and grinning.

## What I changed from the row, in all three

- **The scare leaves the bus stop.** ep11 was a night bus, so the walk stays inside the fairground: puddles, the bumper cars, a shut ticket booth, the fry van.
- **No sign with words.** "ALL GUESTS MUST EXIT TOGETHER" is real words, and the README wants made-up words or none. The maze front has no lettering. C needs a sign, so it's a picture.
- **No grab.** The rig can't grip, so each pitch gets her out a different way, with no held hand.
- **He's "the maze guy", never "the attendant".** Row 1's creature is the Attendant.
- **The panes are windows, not real mirrors.** On the way back in, every pane shows Tasha and none of them show me. The designer can build that as a mirrored copy of the corridor behind clear glass, with the one copy model instanced where my reflection would stand, each running my acting half a beat late. It's cheaper than deep mirror bounces, and it's the same model in every pane by construction.

## A. "Two laughs" (the row's story, sharpened)

**First line:** `Me and Tasha / have a THING. / Whoever says / PICKLE first / buys the FRIES.` Then `And I have / NEVER / got her. / Not ONCE.`
- It opens on the two of us under the fry van's awning in the rain, the lit maze behind, Tasha holding her hand out for fries money.

**Beats** (about 2:20)
1. **0 to 20 s:** the joke, then the fair. The maze guy says ten minutes, then it's coming down.
2. **20 to 42 s:** we split up for a laugh among a hundred Tashas. I hear her laugh ahead of me and behind me at the same time. I come out first, for once in my life. She comes out a second later, shaking off the rain.
3. **42 to 78 s:** walking across the fair she's half a beat behind me. I laugh and she laughs, late. At the fry van I say PICKLE on purpose, to give her free fries. She says "pickle" back, half a beat late, and doesn't hold out her hand. Tasha always holds out her hand. In the puddles, my reflection walks with me and hers is a step behind her.
4. **78 to 108 s:** the scare, then the twist.
5. **108 to 136 s:** back in, and she comes out.
6. **136 to 145 s:** the fry van, and the last line.

**Plants** (each one re-reads at the twist)
- Two laughs at once, one ahead of me and one behind. One was the copy and one was her.
- "For once, I came out FIRST." It sounds like a win, but she never came out at all.
- The maze guy's "ten minutes". It's why a panel is already out when I get back.

**The scare,** at the dark window of a shut ticket booth. It builds in six steps.
1. Two soft taps on glass. I look round and there's nobody. Tasha's beside me, watching the rain.
2. In the booth window there's my reflection, and next to it Tasha, hood down and soaked, standing in a corridor of mirrors that isn't behind us. She's mouthing my name.
3. I look beside me. It's Tasha, hood up, dry, watching me.
4. Back to the window. The soaked one points at her and shakes her head.
5. The one beside me turns to the window, half a beat late, then back to me.
6. My face goes scared. Then hers does, half a beat later, like she's checking how it goes.
- The music drops out under steps 5 and 6, leaving rain and the voice. The hit lands on the copied face.

**The twist:** `We never / came out / TOGETHER.` / `Tasha was / still IN there.`

**Going back in**
- I run back. When I look back, the booth is empty.
- The maze guy has half the panels out, the roof tarp is off and rain falls straight in. Every pane shows Tasha and none show me. They all move when I move, half a beat late.
- I shout PICKLE. Every one of them says it back, late. Except one, who laughs and yells FRIES. That's the proof: an echo can only hand my word back, and only Tasha claims her fries.
- The panel next to her is already out, and the maze guy is loading it onto the truck with his back to us. She steps through the empty frame, soaked, and bumps my shoulder.
- In the pane she left, one Tasha stays, half a beat behind us. Then the maze lights fade off slowly and that pane goes dark.

**The warm turn, shown:** the fry van's warm strip light, the only warm colour in the film. A two-shot side by side, her hair flat with rain and the shine back in her eyes. I put coins on the counter a few at a time, and she keeps her hand out for more. The score turns to the major for a few bars.

**Last line:** `I yelled PICKLE / the whole way / through that MAZE.` / `That's a LOT / of FRIES.` Then a cut on picture to the first shot and "Me and Tasha have a THING."

**Risks**
- **Flashing:** the booth window is dark, so the scare is safe. In the maze, check every shot at the final's own frame steps, never pan across reflected bulbs, and make the lights-off one slow fade.
- **Harm:** the soaked Tasha in the window looks urgent, never crying or terrified, and never pressed to the glass. The taps are two light ones with a hand. They're never a pattern like ep12's knocks, and nobody answers them.
- **Batch 1 echoes:** the copy saying "pickle" back is a word copied, which is near ep11's "In MY VOICE". It's one word and the find needs it, but standards should hear it. Never say "from the inside", which is ep11's handprint line and row 4's first line.

## B. "Out of sight" (it can only copy what it can see)

**First line:** `Tasha points / at anything GREEN / and goes, / what's THAT?` Then `And I say / PICKLE. / Every TIME.` Then `Whoever / says PICKLE / buys the FRIES.`
- It opens side-on on Tasha pointing at my green ballcap under the maze's glow. The cap is a plain one of ours and copies no catalogue item.

**Beats** (about 2:25)
1. **0 to 18 s:** the joke, happening. Then the fair, where the maze guy is already rolling the roof tarp back. He doesn't say a word.
2. **18 to 38 s:** we split up among a hundred Tashas, all moving except one far down the glass, standing still. I figured it was the angles. I come out and she comes out after me, her hair parted on the other side. I figured it was the wind.
3. **38 to 60 s:** walking out she's half a beat behind me, always on the side where the puddles are. I stop and she stops, late. I try her own trick on her. I point at my cap and go, what's that? She points at her own head and goes, what's that? Half a beat late. She never says pickle, and she never yells fries.
4. **60 to 75 s:** I duck behind the bumper cars' booth for a second. When I come out she's frozen mid-step, one foot up, rain running off her hood. Then she sees me and does it all: the step, the duck, the step. Then she's half a beat behind me again.
5. **75 to 108 s:** the scare, then the twist.
6. **108 to 138 s:** back in the maze. The find, the proof and the way out.
7. **138 to 148 s:** the fry van, and the last line.

**Plants**
- The one Tasha standing still on the first trip. That was her, the only thing in there not copying anyone, and at the find she's in the same spot.
- Her hair parted on the other side, flipped the way a mirror flips it. The copy is Tasha's rig mirrored left to right, so this comes for free.
- My green cap and her trick, from the first line. Starting the joke is the one thing a copy can't do.

**The scare,** behind the tarp on the bumper cars, out of her sight.
1. I stand back there a long time. I'm shaking. I put my hands over my face and take three breaths. Cut to her out on the open ground in the rain, frozen with one hand half up.
2. I step out. She's exactly where I left her, still frozen. Then she sees me.
3. The music drops out. She does all of it at once, in about two seconds: the shaking, the hands over the face, the three breaths and the step out. She snaps from pose to pose, too fast for a person, in one held side-on shot with no cuts.
4. She stops in my pose, facing me the way a reflection would, half a beat behind me again. Her eyes are black like a window at night, with nothing in them and no shine at all.
- The sound is silence, then the burst as a tight run of cloth snaps and footfalls, then silence again, then the rain coming back.

**The twist:** `That's what / a REFLECTION does.` / `It can't MOVE / if it can't / SEE you.` / `She came out / of the GLASS.` / `Tasha was / still IN there.`

**Back in the maze**
- I run back, and it runs at my shoulder, half a beat behind. Half the panels are gone and rain is falling in. Every pane shows Tasha copying me.
- **The find:** I step behind a stacked steel frame where nothing can see me. Every Tasha freezes, and so does the one at my shoulder. Except one, far down the glass in the same spot as before, waving both arms.
- **The proof:** I step out. She points at my green cap and mouths, what's THAT? A copy can't start anything. I say PICKLE, and she yells FRIES. She's still Tasha, and she's still winning.
- **The way out, with no hands:** the copy keeps to my puddle side out there, and my glass side in here. So in front of her pane it's between me and the glass, facing me, the way a reflection stands. I step back. It steps back too, half a beat late, and goes right into her pane. Cut, and Tasha is stepping out of that same pane, soaked, with the copy in the glass behind her. Nobody is seen crossing.
- The maze guy carries that panel to the truck with his back to us.

**The warm turn, shown:** the fry van, side by side. We look up at the rain at the same moment, and nobody's late. I've got the money out before she even points.

**Last line:** `At the / fry VAN, / she pointed / at my CAP.` Then a cut on picture into the first shot, "Tasha points at anything GREEN", so the loop is the joke starting again.

**Risks**
- **Flashing:** the burst moves a big bright yellow coat fast. Keep it side-on at mid-shot, well under a quarter of the frame, with no cuts inside it, and measure it at the final's frame step. The maze shots follow A's rules.
- **The rig:** the burst is about eight poses in two seconds, with acting keys every 0.25 s. Hands over the face means raised arms, staged side-on so they don't read as beams. It needs a 3 s test render next to the creature test still, before the build.
- **Contact:** the burst ends close to me, so probe it. The gap is shown and never said.
- **A mirrored rig** is a small step from the row's "exactly her rig". The producer should OK it, and the designer should check the mirrored mesh's normals and the face texture.
- **ep11's rule has the same shape.** ep11 says it only moved when the lights went out, and B says it can't move if it can't see you. The picture is the opposite, because she freezes in plain sight in the light and never jumps closer. Still, keep the words apart and never use "only moves when".

## C. "First" (one of them is facing the wrong way)

**First line:** `Whoever says / PICKLE first / pays for FRIES.` Then `Tasha has / NEVER / PAID.` Then `She'd rather / walk home / in the RAIN.`
- It opens on Tasha at the fry van with her arms folded, grinning, while I count coins.

**Beats** (about 2:20)
1. **0 to 20 s:** the joke and her streak, then the fair. Her yellow raincoat has the little fry patch I sewed on her left sleeve. The sign by the exit is only a picture of two people walking out side by side.
2. **20 to 40 s:** we split up. In one mirror I see Tasha where I should be, and I blame the angles. I come out, and she comes out half a beat after.
3. **40 to 60 s:** at the fry van she doesn't even try to make me say it. She always tries. She just stands there doing what I do, half a beat behind. Then I see the patch. It's on her other sleeve, and there's no shine in her eyes.
4. **60 to 66 s:** the twist. I go back, and it comes with me.
5. **66 to 100 s:** the scare, inside the maze.
6. **100 to 132 s:** the find, the proof, and we walk out together.
7. **132 to 145 s:** the fry van, and the last line.

**Plants**
- The fry patch on her left sleeve. On the copy it's on the right.
- The picture sign of two people walking out side by side. We came out apart, and walking out together is how she gets out.
- "She'd rather walk home in the rain." At the proof she gives up the one thing she never gives up.
- Tasha in the mirror where I should be. That's the maze's rule for the whole second half.

**The twist:** `The patch / was on / her OTHER sleeve.` / `Like in / a MIRROR.` / `Tasha was / still IN there.` The late turn is the proof below.

**The scare,** inside the maze, with the roof half off and rain falling in.
1. No pane shows me. Every one of them shows Tasha, copying me half a beat late.
2. The copy walks at my shoulder, half a step behind, and I can't get ahead of it. I walk faster and it's there. I stop and it stops.
3. In the glass, every Tasha has another Tasha at her shoulder, half a beat behind her. Then another behind that one, all the way down the glass, each one later than the last.
4. I spin round to face the copy, fast. It's a hard cut, never a whip pan.
5. For half a beat it hasn't turned yet. It's still staring ahead at nothing, right next to my face. Then it turns to me, late. Face to face, its eyes are black glass with nothing behind them. The music drops, then the hit.
6. Down the glass, every Tasha turns to face me, later and later the further away she is.

**Finding her**
- **The find:** every Tasha in the glass is facing me. Except one, with her back to me. The wrong way.
- **The proof:** I don't shout, because anything I say they'll say after me. So I stand still and say nothing, and every Tasha waits for me. Then the one with her back to me turns round on her own and says PICKLE. First. Before me. Tasha has never said it first, and she gave up her streak so I'd know it was her.
- **The way out, with no hands, is the sign.** We walk out together, me in the corridor and her in the glass right beside me, step for step and on time, all the way to the exit. At the door she's next to me, out in the rain.
- In the last mirror by the door, one Tasha watches us go, half a beat late. The maze guy shuts the maze off with one slow fade.

**The warm turn, shown:** the fry van. She puts her money on the counter. I just stare, and she shrugs. The van's steel side shows our two reflections, side by side. When she bumps my shoulder, her reflection bumps mine at the same moment, and nobody's late.

**Last line:** `She PAID. / First time EVER.` Then a cut on picture into "Whoever says PICKLE first pays for FRIES."

**Risks**
- **Flashing and render cost:** C spends the most time in the maze. Use the windows-not-mirrors build and check every maze shot at the final's frame steps. The spin is a cut, never a pan across bulbs.
- **Shape:** the copy is known at about 60 s, earlier than batch 1's twists. The maze has to carry the middle, and the late turn is the proof, where she gives up the streak.
- **The first line is rule-shaped.** "Whoever says X pays" is closer to batch 1's openers than A or B. A fallback that keeps the loop is `Tasha has / NEVER paid / for a FRY.` / `Whoever says / PICKLE first / PAYS.`
- **The face beside mine at the spin** is near ep11's "Its head came down BESIDE mine". Keep it side by side and level with a clear gap, never bending down to me.
- **The turning down the glass** is a cascade, like ep13's eyes opening down the rows. Keep it to one quick sweep, and never use ep13's words.
- **A mirrored rig** flips the patch. That's the same producer OK and the same normals check as in B.
- **Walking out together must not read as row 3's guiding.** Nobody carries a light, and she isn't following me. We walk side by side.

## Risks all three share

- **Flashing:** the maze bulbs never chase or strobe, nothing pans across reflected bulbs, and the only light change is one slow fade. Every mirror shot gets the flash pre-check at the final's own frame steps.
- **Harm:** Tasha is "still in there". Her face in the glass is urgent or grinning, never crying or terrified.
  - Words to keep out of every line: lost, missing, trapped, taken, took, pulled, grab, scream, fell, "dead end", "dead still", "shooting gallery".
  - `check` blocks "dead" and "shooting" outright, and the rest are on its harm list.
- **Text:** there's no lettering on the maze, the booth or the van, and no price board. Coins are plain discs, and C's sign has no words.
- **Not for kids:** no fairgoers at all, and no kiddie rides. That means no carousel, teacups or hook-a-duck.
  - A yellow raincoat in the rain is close to IT's Georgie. So there are no storm drains, paper boats, balloons or clowns anywhere, and Tasha has to read clearly adult.
- **The film *Us* (2019)** opens on a double in a seaside hall of mirrors, and its twist is a swap. Doubles in mirrors are old folklore, and our place, cast, joke and ending are our own.
  - None of its images go in, such as a boardwalk or beach, red jumpsuits, scissors, rabbits or a "find yourself" sign.
  - The packager should keep the title away from it.

## My pick: B, "Out of sight"

- **Its middle really escalates, and only this creature could do it.** First she's late. Then she's frozen in the rain when she can't see me. Then she replays everything I did in two seconds and stops in my pose. Nothing in batch 1 or the other three rows does that. The README's first lesson about the story is that the middle has to scare by escalating, and this one does.
- **The viewer learns the rule before the scare, and the ending runs on it.** The freeze is shown at 60 s and again at the scare. Then the find (I hide and she's the one who keeps moving) and the way out (I back the copy into her pane) both use it. Nobody explains anything, and nobody needs a hand to grip.
- **It has the fewest batch-1 echoes.** It has no knocks and no glass "from the inside", and the only copied words are one mimed question. A carries all three.
- **Its loop is the cleanest.** The last line, "she pointed at my CAP", is the first line's picture, so the cut back reads as the joke starting over.
- **The cost:** the burst needs a 3 s test render next to the creature test still, and the maze is visited twice.
- **If the producer wants more of a lump in the throat,** C's proof is the warmest beat in this file: she says PICKLE first and gives up her streak. It only fits B if B's first line changes to her streak, so I'd keep B as written and leave that as a choice for the brief.
