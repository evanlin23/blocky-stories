## The big idea
**Grandma's sixty-year-old recipe card is in my handwriting because I write it tonight: when I finally cook her soup right, my copy flies out the window and lands sixty years back in nineteen-year-old Grandma's soup pot, so nobody ever invented the soup. She learned it from my card, and I learned it from hers.**

- **Engine:** the bootstrap loop (premise-engines engine 2), one object and one jump, as the panel asked. No film's object, traveler or scene.
- **Why it clicks ("oh, THAT's why"):** the handwriting is mine because it is mine. The boot-shaped stain over the last line is the drip off my spoon tonight. "You taught me this" every Sunday was never a joke. The card is older than me because it's the same card, sixty years further on. Grandma never cooks with the card in her hand because she knows the rule and knows the card isn't done yet. She says "it's tonight, isn't it" because she's been waiting sixty years for this Sunday.
- **The secret step closes the loop too:** the stain hides the last line, so everyone who holds the card has to find the last step themselves. Grandma found it at nineteen, I find it tonight, and it's the thing I put on everything: lemon. So the soup always tasted a little like me.
