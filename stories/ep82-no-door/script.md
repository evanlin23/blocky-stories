## The big idea
**The house with no door isn't a house at all: it's somebody enormous sitting very still with a roof on, and one plain pizza a night, slid through the slot that is its mouth, is what keeps it sitting.**

- Ten pitched, this one kept. ## The story
- **Premise:** Every night at six I bring one large plain pizza to a lemon house with no door and no path, and slide it through a brass slot at knee height. A twenty slides back out, folded into a tiny house. The whole street keeps its porch lights on until it's delivered. My big sister Marisol ran this route for ten years and gave it to me this month. Tonight I get a flat tire two blocks away.
- **Rule / hook (L01, about 2.3 s):** "I bring PIZZA every night / to a house / with no DOOR." L02 tightens it to now: "And TONIGHT, / I'm LATE."
- **The relatable want:** not messing up the job your big sister trusted you with.
- **The marker (the rule meter), on screen by frame 1 and in every shot:** a round tomato-red delivery timer badge in the top-left corner, our own design (a pizza-slice glyph and digits only). It counts down to 6:00 in the montage, then counts up in red from L14: +0:01, +0:02, +0:03. At L37 it resets to a green check.
- **The 3 to 5 example montage (L03 to L10, 3 to 18 s):** one large plain at six (L03), no door and no path, a brass slot at knee height (L04), the slot warm like breath (L05), the twenty folded into a tiny house (L06, L07), the chimney puffing every four seconds (L08), porch lights on until it's delivered (L09), nobody walking a dog past it (L10).
- **A concrete mystery on screen by 15 s:** the doorless house, the warm slot and the folded twenty (L04 to L07, by 12 s).
- **How the narrator is the exception:** everyone on the street stays behind their porch lights. I'm the one who walks up to the slot.
- **The loved one at stake:** my big sister, Marisol, named at L11 in the same shot as her at the pizza place on the corner. At L28, L29 the house turns toward her lit door.
- **The rule broken:** L14 to L16, about 24 s in: late, for the first time.
- **First real scare:** L19 to L23 (30 to 35 s): the top windows blink, the lawn splits, the house stands up.
- **The climax turns the rule's own logic:** the order always says the same thing, "the house with no door", an address and never a person. So I deliver it there: to the bare dirt where the house used to sit. It comes home and sits down on top of it (L30 to L38). No new magic.
- **Twist and reveal (L23 to L27, 34 to 41 s):** said plainly. "The whole house / was SOMEBODY. / Sitting very STILL, / with a ROOF on." Then one picture each for the knees and the slot that is its mouth.
- **How the last 15 seconds turn over the first line:** warm on the curb with Marisol, then the sting: last night a second order came in, from another house with no door (L43). There's another one sitting somewhere on my route. The last line ("So rain or SHINE,") runs into L01.

## World
One place: a short dead-end street at sundown, seen from the curb in front of the doorless house. Painted blocky houses with porches and porch lights (mint, sky blue, lilac), white picket fences, a hydrant. At the corner, the pizza place: a small red-and-white building with a big front window, a red door with a warm light over it, a plain red delivery car (no lettering). The doorless house sits in its own lot halfway down: two storeys, lemon-yellow brick `#ffe14d`, a white roof edge, a red brick chimney, two upstairs windows with no curtains, one downstairs bay window, no door, no path, no mailbox, a neat green lawn, and at knee height in the front wall one worn brass slot with a hinged flap.
- **The house when it stands:** the lawn splits along two lines and two huge blocky knees in lemon-yellow brick push up, then the whole house rises on two thick brick legs, the downstairs bay window turning out to be its chest and the slot its mouth, the two top windows its eyes, the roof its hat. Its arms are the two side walls, unfolding down. Built from our blocks, wrong in one dimension: size (about 9 m standing). It stands, turns its upper half toward the corner, and never walks on screen.
- **No text anywhere.** The badge, the car, the box (a plain kraft box with a red slice glyph) and the twenty (plain green, no numbers or faces, folded into a tiny house shape).
- **What's true underneath (never said beyond the reveal):** it has sat in that lot since before the street. Fed at six, it stays sitting. Late, it stands and turns toward the nearest lit door. The pizza goes in its lot, never into any person's hands. It never touches or takes anyone.
