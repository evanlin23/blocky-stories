Verdict: revise

Fiction, kids and mature themes, originality and package pass. Preview flash PASS.

## Must-fix
1. **IP, the Skater's face (`set.py` `skater_face`; S22 to S24, S34, S38 to S41, S49 to S56).** It has two solid black upright ovals with white highlights and a thin upturned arc on a plain cream head. At phone size (stills f01235, f01775, f02444) it reads as the platform's default smile face, which rule 4 forbids. Redesign it as our own painted mascot face: for example, round eyes with a cherry rim and a cream iris ring, a wide two-tone painted grin with a hard lower edge, and faded cherry cheek dots. Keep L38 true ("two dark eyes and a painted smile"), and re-run the creature test still.
2. **Flash risk, S10 (L09, 16.6 to 18.2 s).** The pan across the high-contrast cherry and cream stripes is the film's worst moment: 2 flashes and 4 changes a second at 17.27 s. That is right at the house bar on a quarter-res preview, and the final's full frame rate can tip it over. Lock the camera off for S10, or halve the pan and lower the stripe contrast in that shot. Then re-run the flash pre-check on S09 to S11 at the final's frame step.
