## Must-fix
1. **Flash, S55 (L50, 96.7 to 97.0 s).** A dark cherry block passes through the lens mid-shot. The frame goes from luma 94 to 40 to 18, then back to 81, inside 0.27 s. This near-blackout and recovery is now the film's worst flash moment, sitting on the house bar. Keep the camera ([2.3, -6.9, 1.0]) clear of every body and arm for the whole shot (move it back or move the passer), so nothing crosses the lens. Re-run the flash pre-check on S54 to S56 at the final's frame step.
2. **Rule 2, S77 to S78 (L72 to L73, 137 to 140 s).** The overhead still looks top-down. Pop and Nan read as one pale shape lying flat on the boards, in a pool of light. On an old couple's "last skate", that reads as two people collapsed, which feeds a death reading of the twist. Tilt the camera to at least 35 degrees off vertical so you see legs, skates and two upright bodies, and part them a body width with joined hands. No figure should ever look like it is lying down.
