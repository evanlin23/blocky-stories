## The big idea
**The rink DJ will skip any song you ask, but he skips you instead: you land at the start of the next song with your fries cold. Skipped time folds up into a double-jointed skater that comes after you, one fold for every song you skipped, and when it reaches you, every skipped minute lands on you in the same second and folds you up the way it's folded, into the next Skater.**

- Ten pitched, this one kept (pitch 2, "Skip Ahead"). Dropped: the rink keeps the couple who met there (romance), the spotlight shows who you'll lose (sad), the hire skates walk you to whoever wore them last (ghost), the fans turn time backward (time running differently, ep33's shelf), the rink is a record and we're the groove (abstract), the snack bar serves the hour you're hungry for (ep83's shelf), the Skater is your future self (about-me, twelve stories), the rail holds everyone who ever waited (forgotten, crowded), Lenny is the rink (gag), every lap is a year (altitude time again).
- Checked against "Claimed big ideas" and every twist: not forgotten or unseen, not a stand-in, not time running differently in a place (time here doesn't run slower or faster. It's spent later), not a reflection or a copy. A fast-forward with a price that comes to collect.
- **The rule is the clue:** "he skips YOU" first reads as a fun power, a skip button for life. After the reveal it's literal and has a bill: the minutes were yours, and they're still coming.

## The story
- **Premise:** My best friend, Gus, has his birthday skate at the rink, and I know nobody else there. Lenny the DJ will skip any song you ask, and when he does, you skip: you're at the start of the next song somewhere else on the floor, the clock has jumped and your fries are cold. I skip everything awkward: my wipeout, the small talk, the couples skate, Gus's speech. Each time the spotlight sweeps in for a new song, a Skater in faded cherry-and-cream stripes stands nearer, and one more of its limbs bends twice.
- **Rule / hook (L01, 12 words, about 2.4 s):** "If you ask the DJ / to SKIP a song, / he skips YOU." L02 tightens it: "The song keeps PLAYING. / You just MISS it."
- **The relatable want:** getting out of awkward moments at a party where you only know one person.
- **The rule meter, on screen the whole time: the song lights.** A row of twelve round bulbs along the front of the DJ booth, one per song tonight, our own design, no text. A played song's bulb glows cream. The song playing now glows warm gold. A skipped song's bulb glows cherry. Each skip lights the next bulb cherry (ramp 0.4 s). Each song taken back turns one cherry bulb cream (ramp 0.6 s). The number of cherry bulbs always equals the Skater's extra bends. Every wide and floor shot has the booth and its lights in frame. Close shots carry the lights in soft focus behind, or their glow and reflection on the floor's shine or the chrome of the snack bar. Beside the booth hangs the rink clock, a big round face with hands only, no numerals, so every jump reads.
- **The 3 to 5 example montage, done on me (L04 to L13, first 30 s):** the wipeout skipped (L04 to L07), then small talk skipped in one line (L10), the second skip landing straight on the Skater nearer (L13).
- **A concrete threat on screen by 15 s:** the Skater, first seen at about 13.5 s in the background of the L06 snack-bar shot as the spotlight sweeps the far rail, then named at L08.
- **How the narrator is the exception:** everyone else lives their songs. I skip mine.
- **The loved one:** my best friend, Gus (named at L03 in the same shot as him, crown on). Friendship, not family.
- **The rule broken:** it's broken by using it. Each skip is the rule at work, and each one brings the Skater a step nearer.
- **First real scare:** L19 to L22 (about 41 to 51 s). I skip Gus's speech, and the Skater is right beside me at the rail, folded tight, its double-bent arm held out to me.
- **Twist and reveal (L23 to L25, about 51 to 60 s), said plainly:** "I COUNTED. / FOUR skips. / FOUR extra bends." "That's where skipped time GOES. / It folds UP / and comes AFTER you, / one FOLD / for every song / you SKIPPED." - **The climax turns the rule's own logic (L26 to L35):** if skipped songs land all at once and fold you up, take them back one at a time, small enough to live through. Lenny's back button (planted at L05, "SKIP, / or BACK?") is the only way the rule allows. I yell "BACK!", and I live each one, and each song taken back unfolds one joint and turns one cherry bulb cream. No new magic.
- **The warm turn (shown):** in the wipeout I'd skipped, Gus went down with me on purpose, laughing. In the speech I'd skipped, Gus was talking about me: "I always go down FIRST, so nobody else feels DUMB." Warm, funny, not sad.
- **How the last 15 seconds turn over the first line:** the Skater stands straight, a head taller than everyone, and the spotlight passes and it's gone. Then the sting: just now, a guy asked Lenny for a skip, and at the far rail something folded once. L38 ("So I told HIM,") runs into L01, so the opening rule plays as my warning to him, and to you.
- **The plants a rewatch catches:**

  2. **Nearer with every skip (L14):** "Every time I SKIPPED, / it was CLOSER." And it's folded tighter each time (shorter in frame), so at L21 it only comes up to my shoulder.

  4. **The wipeout (S04b, unnarrated):** as I go down, Gus is already tipping down beside me, laughing, and the skip cuts before he lands. L27 pays it off.
- **Ending shape:** a debt paid back in pieces, a straight-standing reveal that clears, and a cold sting that loops.

## World
One bright roller rink on a Saturday afternoon, a birthday skate. A maple floor polished to honey, a waist-high padded rail in cherry red with cream posts, cherry-and-cream stripes painted round every wall, three ceiling fans turning slowly, the double doors propped open on a bright blue afternoon sky. A snack bar with chrome stools and an amber lamp, a hire counter with a wall of wooden cubbies full of skates, and a raised DJ booth with a soft lamp, the song lights along its front, the skip button on its desk and the clock on the wall beside it. Every light is up for fast songs. The only moving light is one soft spotlight that sweeps the floor at the start of every song. No glitter ball. No text anywhere: no signs, no names on shirts, no logos on skates or cups, no numerals on the clock.
- **What's true underneath (never said beyond the reveal):** every skipped song is still yours. Lenny's button doesn't delete it. It folds it up and leaves it on the floor, and the folds add up into one skater that walks the time back to you, a step nearer at each new song. Taken back a song at a time, it's just a song. Taken all at once, every minute lands in one second and folds you into the next skater.
