## The big idea
**Every grape you ever tasted in this store is on a tab in the back, the whole town works its tabs off overnight (that's who stocks the shelves), and my tab of 4,106 grapes isn't mine: it's every grape Grandma slipped me from the bag, one an aisle, every Thursday since I could sit in the cart.**

- **Engine:** the bill comes due (a ledger kept on you that you never knew about), our own version. No film's plot, place or people.
- **Why it clicks ("oh, THAT's why"):** nobody answers the page because everybody has a tab. The shelves are full every morning and nobody's ever seen who stocks them: it's the mailman and my dentist in green aprons. I've "never stolen so much as a grape", and I'm paged anyway. Grandma said "keep walking" because she knew whose grapes they were.

## The story
- **Premise:** At this supermarket, if they page your name at closing, you don't go to the back, because that's where they keep your tab. I drive my grandma, Bev, every Thursday at closing. At 9:59 the speakers say my name. I've never stolen a grape, so I go to the back.
- **Rule / hook (L01, about 2.6 s):** "If they page your NAME / at closing, / don't go to the BACK." L02 tightens it: "That's where they keep / your TAB."
- **The relatable want or fight:** driving Grandma to the store when she takes forever and talks to the melons.
- **The marker (the rule meter), on screen by frame 1 and in every shot:** a round orange price sticker inset in the top-left corner, our own design, showing a grape glyph and a running number (my tab). It reads 0 until L14, jumps to 4106 at L28, and drops to 90 by L42, then ticks to 91 at L44.
- **The 3 to 5 example montage (L03 to L09, 4 to 15 s):** a grape tasted in produce (L03), a free sample taken twice (L04), the pen on the chain walked off with (L05), shoppers shopping faster when the page comes (L07, L08), and full shelves every morning with no stocker ever seen (L09).
- **A concrete mystery on screen by 15 s:** shelves full every morning and nobody's ever seen who stocks them (L09, at 13 to 15 s).
- **How the narrator is the exception:** nobody answers the page. I go.
- **The loved one at stake:** my grandma, Bev, named at L10 in the same shot as her at the cart.
- **The rule broken:** L17, about 26 s.
- **First real scare:** L18 to L28 (28 to 43 s): the back goes on forever, half the town in green aprons stocking in silence, and the Tallyman steps out, beeps at my face and reads my tab.
- **The climax turns the rule's own logic:** a tab gets worked off, and the rule never says by whom, so Grandma and I split it (L36 to L40). No new magic.
- **Twist and reveal (L29 to L35, 44 to 55 s):** said plainly. "Every grape was HER." "Every Thursday since I could SIT in the cart, she slipped me one from the BAG. Just ONE. Every AISLE." "The whole town owes for SNACKS."
- **How the last 15 seconds turn over the first line:** we're down to ninety grapes, and tonight in aisle three Grandma slipped me just one more (L42 to L44). "So if you've ever had just one grape," (L45) runs into L01.
- **The plants a rewatch catches:**
