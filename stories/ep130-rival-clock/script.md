**Everyone's sleeve counts down to their best friend, and mine hit zero eleven years ago on the morning I met Gus, the guy I've fought with every day since. The clocks count down to the day someone becomes your best friend, and Gus's reads ONE: his has spent eleven years counting down to me.**
