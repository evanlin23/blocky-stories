## The big idea (one sentence)
Our neighbor's mower makes the grass longer because Mr. Odell lives backward through time: our tomorrow is his yesterday, so he isn't guessing our future, he remembers it, and in my dad's future the two of them are best friends.

- **The "wait, what":** the neighbor isn't psychic and the lawn isn't magic. We're watching a normal man mow a normal lawn, played in reverse. Every strange thing on the street is ordinary life running the other way.
- **How it clicks (every early detail, one reason):**

  - His grass gets shorter by noon, by itself (L06): that's grass growing, rewound.
  - So the lawn is the SAME every morning, which is why it looks "perfect" (L29, said once).
  - He mows with his eyes shut (L07) because he's already seen every pass (L30, said once). The lawn is the same every night.

  - He knows Dad's coffee order before Dad orders (L13 to L15): he's bought it for Dad for years. Dad hasn't lived those years yet.

## The story
- **Premise:** I moved back home with my dad this spring. Next door, Mr. Odell mows at 3 AM with his eyes shut, and his mower makes the grass longer. The street's rule: never look at his lawn while it's running. Dad has lived next door to him for six years and they've never said a word, but Mr. Odell waves at Dad like he misses him, and at the coffee cart he knows Dad's order before Dad says it. Dad, a grump with no new friends, decides it's a trick and sits up to catch him.
- **Rule:** never look at Mr. Odell's lawn while his 3 AM mower runs.
- **Twist:** Mr. Odell lives backward. Our tomorrow is his yesterday. He remembers our future, and in it he and Dad are best friends. The friendship is in Dad's FUTURE and in Mr. Odell's past (never the other way: the cold check failed an earlier wording that put it in Dad's past).
- **Plants (a rewatch catches both):**
  - Plant 1, S01 (frame 1, 0 s) and S05 (L05): green clippings flying up INTO the mower deck and sticking back onto the stems. First watch: a weird magic lawn. Rewatch: a rewind.
  - Plant 2, L11 and L13 to L15 (about 17 to 28 s): he waves at Dad like he misses him, then knows Dad's coffee order before Dad has ordered. First watch: creepy neighbor. Rewatch: an old friend.
  - Bonus plant, L13: the hot chocolate in Mr. Odell's hand at the cart (pays off at L37 to L40).
- **The relatable want:** my dad hasn't made a friend in years and won't admit he'd like one. The fight is the dad-vs-neighbor grudge everyone has seen on their street.
- **The narrator is the witness, Dad is the exception:** the whole street shuts its blinds at three. Dad is the one who looks.
- **The loved one at stake:** my dad, and whether he lets the friendship happen.
- **The loophole the climax turns:** the rule's own logic. If Mr. Odell already remembers the friendship, it still needs a first day in Dad's time, and nobody can start it but Dad. Dad crosses the fence with two coffees. No new magic.
- **How the last 15 seconds turn over line 1:** line 1 is a goofy what-if about a lawn. By L42 the "longer grass" is the visible edge of a man living the other way through his best friend's life, and today was the last day of it for him. The last line, "Just ask yourself," runs into line 1, which now reads as a real question with a real answer.
- **Ending shape:** a loop on a bittersweet sting after a warm crossing. Warmth: friendship between neighbors, shown (Dad stepping over the fence with two cups, Mr. Odell opening his eyes).
