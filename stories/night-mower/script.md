## The story
- **Premise:** I live with my dad again. Next door, Mr. Odell mows at 3 AM with his eyes shut, and his mower makes the grass longer. The street's rule: never look at his lawn while it's running. Every blind snaps shut, and the people who looked won't say what they saw. Dad and Mr. Odell have been neighbors six years and never said a word, but Mr. Odell waves at Dad like he misses him, and the first words he ever says to Dad are Dad's coffee order, before Dad orders. Dad sits up to catch him.
- **Rule:** never look at Mr. Odell's lawn while his 3 AM mower runs. **The consequence (r2):** while it runs, time on that lawn runs his way, and whoever watches sees a piece of tomorrow.
- **Twist:** Mr. Odell lives backward. Our tomorrow is his yesterday. He remembers our future, and in Dad's future he and Dad are best friends. The friendship is in Dad's FUTURE and Mr. Odell's past, never the other way.
- **Plants (a rewatch catches both):**
  - Plant 1, S01 (frame 1, 0 s) and S05 (L05): green clippings flying up INTO the mower deck and sticking back onto the stems.
  - Plant 2, L11 and L12 to L15 (about 17 to 28 s): he waves at Dad like he misses him, then knows Dad's coffee order before Dad has ordered.
  - Plant 3, L07: "the most PERFECT lawn on the street", every morning.
- **The relatable want:** my dad hasn't made a friend in years and won't admit he'd like one.
- **Dad is the exception:** the whole street shuts its blinds at three. Dad is the one who looks.
- **The loved one at stake:** my dad, and whether he lets the friendship happen.
- **The loophole the climax turns:** the rule's own logic. Dad has seen his tomorrow (himself on that lawn with two coffees), and Mr. Odell remembers it, but it still needs a first day in Dad's time, and only Dad can start it. He crosses the fence. No new magic.
- **How the last 15 seconds turn over line 1:** line 1 is a goofy what-if. By L41 the "longer grass" is the edge of a man living the other way through his best friend's life. L42 to L43 pay the rule: Dad looks every night now because he likes what's coming, and the rest of the street keeps its blinds shut "and just WONDER," which runs into line 1 as one sentence.
- **Ending shape:** a loop after a warm crossing and a bittersweet sting. Warmth: friendship between neighbors, shown.
