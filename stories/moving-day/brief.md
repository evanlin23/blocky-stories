## Story

- **Premise:** I'm an adult and I've moved with Mum into a smaller house with low ceilings, because the old one got sold. Mum is tired and hasn't laughed since. Something built from our own moving boxes is standing in a different room each morning. It's taller every time, with a new marker face. Meanwhile the stack by the front door shrinks, and Mum's things turn up in odd places.
- **The rule:** it's the creature's, never spoken as a rule, and nobody has to keep or break it.
  - Each night boxes leave the stack, one more thing goes where it lived in the old house, and by morning it stands in a new room, taller, with a new face.
  - The viewer learns this from the clock wides and the mug by about 30 s.
  - There's no human rule, no warning and no rule broken. That's a deliberate break from batch 1's "rule, then rule broken" shape.
- **The mystery:** what it is, who taped it from the inside, and what its faces are aimed at (nothing, then the back of Mum's head, then Mum's door, then me).
- **Plants** (all land by about 24 s):
  1. The inside tape (L01, L02). It pays off at L33 and in the loop.
  2. Mum's ladder line (L07). It pays off at L59 and L67.
  3. The old-hall smell (L08). It pays off at L60.
  4. The quilt left packed (L09). It pays off at L52 to L54.
  5. The mug and "That's where I always KEPT it" (L10, L11). It pays off at L58.
  6. Mum not looking up (L12). It pays off at L24 and L66.
- **The scare:** at 3 a.m. it stands bent over Mum's bed, its face drawn wide open and its long arms reaching down to her.
- **The twist:** I hit the light, and under its hands is the old quilt, tucked round her shoulders. In the old house her room was the cold one. Everything it moved went where it lived before.
- **The warm turn, shown:** in the morning it's gone and the stack is back. Mum comes out in the quilt, looks up for once, sees her photos on the ceiling and says "That's LADDER HEIGHT". She laughs until she has to sit (P02, picture and score only). Then we unpack the old way together in quick two-shots.
- **The end:** in the hall cupboard there's one box still taped shut, the face box, with a smile drawn on it. It never moves and never changes. The narrator has the last line, and 0.25 s later comes a hard cut to L01.
- **The ending shape:** the warmth is between Mum and me (home comes back), and the creature ends as an unexplained, unopened object. Nobody befriends it, thanks it or answers it, and no line gives it a motive. That's not "kind all along", not a ritual done back, and not a later-night return.

## The creature: the Boxes (one model, built once in `set.py`, posed per shot)

The narration only ever calls it "it". "The Boxes" is the team's name. It's a figure built from our own taped brown moving boxes in R6 proportions: a head, a torso, two arms and two legs, each made of boxes, and every proportion is wrong.
- It's taller than anyone, about 2.86 m straight, so it always stoops or folds under the ceiling.
- Its limbs are far too long. Hanging, its hands reach its knees.
- The boxes sag and bulge, with crushed corners and cut handholds.
- Each hand is the open end of a forearm box, its four flaps splayed like fingers.
- Its face is black marker on its head box, and it's new every time we see it.
- Nothing on it is thinner than a box: no rods, strings or tentacles (note 18).

  - **The base:** `dorian`, root about G2 (`root` 43) and about 74 bpm. ep11 was phrygian at 66, ep12 harmonic minor at 58 and ep13 whole-tone at 50.
  - **The signature: no melody until home comes back.** Drone, pad, a soft `pulse` and one low `choir` carry the dread. The **piano enters for the first time at P02** and plays only in the warm turn. "The piano is the old house." There's no filter sweep (ep13's) and no music box (README).
  - **Sections:**

- **The closing sound has to be audible.** It's the drone's low root alone under L78, measured at -18 dB or louder relative to the voice, with a slight fall into the cut if possible. If it isn't heard on phone speakers, add one low piano note as an opt-in sfx.
- **Silence holes** (`music_drops`, anchored to line ids). In each, music, ambience and footsteps fall and the voice plays on.
  - **H1:** `L36-0.6` to `L36.2`, -40 dB, keeping the `sting`. The Boxes in my room.
  - **H2:** `L44.2` to `L45`, -40 dB. The scrape cuts dead on "STOPPED" and the house goes silent with it.
  - **H3, the main one:** `L49-0.6` to `L51.1`, -45 dB, keeping the cardboard creak and the lamp `click`. "WIDE OPEN" and the reach land with only the voice. P01 is near silence with one creak. The room tone comes back with the light.
- **Ambience:** `room` (small and dry, short tail, about -34 LUFS) throughout, lower at night. A fridge `hum` in kitchen shots only (L17 to L21, L43). No rain, wind, crickets, water, drips, static or drone beds.
- **Footsteps:** `wood`, about -22 dB.
- **Effects:**
  - **New and opt-in:** `tape`, a short packing-tape peel with a sticky crackle, under "PEELED" at L02, about -14 dB. It's not at frame 0, so the film doesn't open on a sound (that's floor-thirteen's first-line type).
  - **New and opt-in:** `scrape`, cardboard dragged on floorboards, a stuttering friction, heard through a wall (`distant`). Under L16 for about 2.2 s, and from L43's hold into L44 until "STOPPED".
  - **New and opt-in, the cardboard creak:** one slow, low, papery flex at P01, about -20 dB. It's cardboard, never wood: lookout's scare is a wooden tower creaking.
  - **Existing kinds:**
    - a soft `thud` on "FROWN" (L04)
    - the `sting` at L36
    - a soft `riser` from L45 into L46
    - the lamp switch `click` at L51, audible
    - the cupboard `door` at L75, soft
  - **Never:** `knock`, `bell`, `glitch`, `static`, `wetclick`, `bubbles`, `squeal`, `glide`, `hum_sweep`, `castors`, or any laugh sound. Mum's laugh is picture and score only.
- **Voice:** Kokoro `am_adam` at 1.03 x 1.45, voice A, for every line (note 18). The per-line `tempo` and `gain_db` values in `lines.json` stand.
- **Master:** -14 LUFS, true peak at or below -1 dBTP.

## Standards risks

- **The bed scare must never read as harm to Mum.**
  - Her face is fully clear of the quilt in every 3 a.m. shot. Her eyes are shut and calm, with a slow breath in `animate()` if it's cheap. Her near arm lies out on top of the quilt, relaxed, so nothing reads as pinned.
  - The quilt is loose and puffy at her shoulders, never drawn tight across her.
  - The face box hangs over the middle of the bed with clear air below it (continuity item 6). It never comes toward her face.
  - The hands touch only the quilt's edge, side-on.
  - The touch words are only "reached" and "tucked", and both land on the quilt.
  - L55 now says she slept through it.
  - Contact-probe every 3 a.m. pose before the final (README).
- **"I hit the LIGHT."** It's the dim lamp on the chest of drawers inside her door, coming up over about 0.8 s. The flash pre-check uses `safety.house_report` at the final's own frame steps (ones here).
- **Other stretches to pre-check:**
  - L44 to L55 (the glow, the shadow, the lamp)
  - the cuts from L61 to L62 and from L63 to L64 (night or dawn into the first sun)
  - P02 to L70 (gold sun, the mug glint)
  - every pan past a bright window (L10, L22, L27, L40)
  - the wet gloss at L20
- **Kid appeal (not made for kids):** moving house with Mum, cardboard and doodled faces can read as family or kids' craft content.
  - Both of us are plainly adults (my height, adult clothes, adult jobs: carrying boxes, making tea).
  - There are no toys, school things, kids' drawings or stickers in any box or room.
  - The faces are drawn wrong (a frown down an empty hall, a stare at a head, far too many eyes, a mouth filling the box). The smile is the only friendly face, and it sits in a cupboard in a cold grade.
  - No music box and no nursery cue. `made_for_kids: false`, and the title and description never target kids.
  - Never the cardboard-robot-costume read (phone-size rules).
- **No harm to a child:** there's no child anywhere, in the cast, in the photos or in a word.
- **Nothing graphic:** no blood, wounds, death or killing words. The tape is peeled, never cut, and no blade appears. Nothing breaks, and nobody is hurt. "The old house got sold" stays unexplained, and the photos never show a third person, so no loss is implied.
- **No age on screen.** The ages live in the cast table only.
- **No words on boxes or in the set.** No brands, no moving-company names, no house number and no street sign. The photos show only us and the old house.
- **Never claimed as true:** no "true story", "this happened" or "real" anywhere. No Roblox name or assets: our own rig, faces, patterns and creature.
- **"Mum":** British spelling, voice A reads it. The `asr` plan is in question 5.

## Kill criteria

- **The test still reads as silly**, a cardboard robot costume or a box pile with a doodle, after the designer's two passes. That's a `craft` stop on the creature. The retry: more human-shaped and more wrong, with a longer neck chain, more stoop, a darker key from below and a face that's less doodle and more wrong (note 18).
- **The tuck reads as harm** (smothering, pinning, a death) on any cold watch, and one round of fixes (more air under the face box, her arm out, her breath visible, a held calm close) doesn't clear it. That's `craft`. The retry is a new 3 a.m. reveal against the same story.
- **The twist doesn't land:** the panel doesn't link the mug, photos and quilt to the old house. Revise first: hold L58 to L60, and make the old house's tall photo bigger in frame. If it still fails, `craft`.
- **Kid appeal** that survives two rounds is `craft`. If it's the premise itself (a family moving house with doodled faces), it's `policy`.
- **A batch-skeleton share** that standards won't clear with wording (the house-with-a-relative overlap with ep12): revise the picture first, then `craft`.
- **The render can't fit 10 h** after the step fallbacks. That's `craft`, and the retry: more holds on fours and fewer moving cameras outside the scare.
