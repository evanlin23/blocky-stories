## The big idea
**The silent reading room's thing is made of every laugh anyone ever swallowed under its dome, it drifts toward whoever is holding in the most, and if it reaches you, you never laugh again. My brother has held in forty days.**
- Engine: held-back feelings that take a body (an old myth shape, no specific work).
- The rule is the clue: "days since you last laughed" reads at first as a fun stat. After the reveal it's a danger meter, and the room's silence is what builds the thing. Every swallowed snort we watch is the thing being fed.
- Checked against "Claimed big ideas" and every twist: no other story has a creature built from what people hold back. Closest is ep47 (lie tags), which is about lies on clothes, not a body made of feelings.

## Premise
In this town a glowing number floats over every head: the days since you last laughed. Almost everyone shows a 0 or a 1. My little brother Tam (an adult, never shown with an age) has finals, and he's crammed for six weeks in the old reading room, where nobody makes a sound. His number says 40. I sit beside him and keep quiet too. One afternoon I notice a reader with no number at all, a person printed in reverse with a grin drawn upside down. When a man swallows a snort, it's a table nearer to Tam and bigger, though I never looked away. When I swallow a laugh, it jumps again.

## Rule
- **The world's rule (L01 to L02):** the number over your head is the days since you last laughed. One laugh rolls it back to zero (L03). Nobody explains it on screen, nobody made it, and it's just how the town is.
- **The place's rule, shown and said once:** under the reading room's glass dome, nobody makes a sound (L07). I break it at L32 to L35.
- **The rule meter:** a number is on screen in every shot. Tam's 40 is the meter of the story and sits in frame (or at a frame edge) in every reading-room shot until L37.

## Twist (and where it's planted)
**The thing is made of swallowed laughs, so the silence is what feeds it, and the laugh the room forbids is the only thing that undoes it.** The first line plays one way first time ("forty is a funny, sad stat about my stressed brother") and another on the loop ("forty days of laughs held in is the thing's whole meal, and the town's number is a warning").
- **Plant 1, the cause shown before it's said (L14 to L16, S24 to S27):** a man two rows over snorts and swallows it. I'm looking straight at the thing ("I didn't blink"), and on a same-framing jump cut it's a table nearer and bigger. It can't be a look-away rule: the only thing that happened was a swallowed laugh. **The repeat (L18 to L19, S30 to S32):** I swallow my own laugh, and on the same kind of cut it jumps again, right beside Tam, bigger again. By 36 s the viewer can say "swallowing laughs feeds it, and it goes to Tam" before L26 to L27 say so.
- **Plant 2 (L12, S21):** its mouth is a big open grin drawn upside down (a story-local decal). It reads as a wrongness first time. L26 pays it off: it's every laugh swallowed in here, turned upside down.
- **Unnarrated (S09):** in the first wide of the room, a dark seated figure is already at the far table in the shadow of the stacks, with nothing over its head.

## How the climax turns the rule (the rule's own logic)
1. The rule as stated: one laugh rolls your number back to zero (L03, shown on the woman's 1 rolling to 0).
2. The viewer has watched swallowed laughs feed it (L14 to L19). The reveal confirms it in one heard clue and three lines: muffled laughing inside it (L25), it's every laugh swallowed in here (L26), it goes to whoever holds in the most (L27), and if it reaches you, you never laugh again (L28). Tam is the most, at 40 (L29).
3. So the fix is the rule itself, no new magic: get Tam to laugh once and he's at zero, no longer the one holding in the most (L31).
4. The only way to get a laugh out of him in that room is to break the room's own rule: I stand up and say our dumbest old joke out loud (L32 to L35).
5. Tam cracks (L36), his 40 rolls to 0 (L37), and laughing is catching: the whole room lets go (L38). Every number rolls to 0 (L39). A thing made of held-in laughs can't hold together in a room where nobody's holding in, so it comes apart, and every laugh it swallowed rises up the dome as a soft echo (L40 to L41).
6. The sting by the same logic: next day the dome is silent again, people are swallowing laughs again, and something new is sitting at the far table (L43 to L44).

## The joke (ours, clean, short)
"Why is the dome so QUIET? / Because if it laughed, it'd CRACK up." (L34 to L35). A glass-dome groaner that brothers would have made up in this room years ago. It sets up L36, "Then he CRACKED."
