## The story

- **Premise:** In this village everyone gets ten words a day, worn as ten glass beads round the neck that go dark one per word. After ten, no more words get through to you, only the voice you love most. I talk all day and mine never go dark. My mom leads the village choir and says I never listen, and this week she keeps thanking me for long talks in her kitchen that I was never at.

## Risks

- **Whisper spellings:** "someone's" may come back as "someones", "Mom's" as "moms", "nobody's" as "nobodys". Fix captions, not the voice. "Ah" may come back as "Oh" or "Ahh": the caption stays "AH". "Earmuffs" should be one word.
- **The bead counts on screen:** Mom's tenth fades in S01, and her ten go dark at breakfast (S32, S33) while only I talk. She's dark in every later shot of a day after breakfast (S34, S37, S39, the concert). On Monday morning at her door (S08) she's lit, because I haven't arrived yet. My cord stays fully lit until S96, fades bead by bead through L59 and L60, and goes fully dark on the tenth fade in S99. The Grin has no cord in any shot. Ron's ten are lit in every shot.
- **The logic holes a viewer might poke:** Can I hear the Grin? Never. My beads are lit in every hall scene, so I only see it and hear her side. Why can the Grin's voice reach her when words can't? It wears the voice she loves, and it was never counted (no beads, no tally stroke). Why is Mom dark every morning? My ten reach her at breakfast. Why doesn't the choir's "ah" pull her back? It isn't my voice (L54). Why don't my words? They're words, and her ten are spent (L55). Why does the Grin stop? It doesn't. It faces front, because she's listening to me again.
- **Harm and child words:** none of `checks.HARM`, `GRAPHIC` or `CHILD` appears (no "son", "missing", "lost", "fell"). Mom is asleep, unhurt.
- **Cold reader:** the adult signals are L17 and L18 (my own place, walking up to Mom's before work). The relationship is named in L06 ("my mom"). No shouting.
- **Rig:** no hand on a cord, door, baton, cup or window. Mom steps away from her stand rather than putting a baton down. Gestures stay at chest height with hands in view.
- **Light:** four spots to pre-check, listed under Palette and light. No flicker, no switch, nothing red. The earmuffs' cranberry is never full-frame.
