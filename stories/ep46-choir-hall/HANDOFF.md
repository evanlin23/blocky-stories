# ep46-choir-hall: dropped again (2026-11-03)

## What was tried
The story was re-imagined as **heard-ten**: "If someone's still TALKING after your tenth bead goes DARK, it isn't THEM." The beads never counted the words you say. They count the words that reach you. After ten, the only voice still able to reach you is the one you love most, and the twelfth singer on the choir riser wears it. I never go dark because I talk over everyone. Mom has been talking to it in my voice all week. My "ah" pulls her back because it's my real voice and it isn't a word. The engine is a misread rule.

- Five pitches were cold-read by fresh agents. All five were understood, and heard-ten was kept as the clearest, with the most reuse of the build.
- The full script passed a cold-viewer test (7/10) and had one fix round: the rule made literal, the "ah" logic, the window scene, and a montage cut to three gags.
- Built, voiced (115.6 s, 104 shots), and `check` is clean. The script, package and build are all in this folder.

## Why it was dropped
**The creature.** The twelfth singer failed two creature tests with Opus critics at 390 px, after the redesign under `docs/research/creature-playbook.md`:
- Try 1 scored 4/10, called "thumb guy" or "giraffe neck": the neck and head were the same tan box.
- Try 2 scored 4/10 from both critics, called "a robot with a tin-can head on a pole" or "thermos head". The flat hair disc reads as a lid and the dark face band as a can label. The square neck reads as a pipe, and the seam reads as a smirk line.

The brief allows no rescue past the last try.

## What a retry needs
- **A new creature concept** from a writer, not another tweak of this one. A person-shape made wrong on the R6 cylinder head keeps reading as an object, and the mouth seam fights playbook rule 8 (never a grin). One option is to keep the idea, since the voice is the threat, and show the twelfth singer only as a silhouette behind the hall's frosted window, or by what it does: Mom's face turned to an empty chair is not allowed (no empty-chair twist), so it would need its own picture.
- The script, cold-read notes and story are sound. Cut the L41 "Its GRIN goes all the way ROUND" beat if the mouth goes.
- Before any proof or preview, redraw the preview. The one in out/ predates the creature changes.

The proof, critic and standards reviews were never run.
