## The big idea
**Houses here build a room for everyone who's ever lived in them. Ours has nine rooms for four people, and the five extra rooms are all mine: every time I moved back home, I'd become somebody new, so the house built a room for each of me. Mom kept every one.**

- **Why it clicks:** Mom keeps the five shut and knocks on each one every night (L08, L09), Dad calls them "the guests" (L10), nobody will say whose they are (L11), I've moved home five times (L12), and Mom always says "your room's ready" without saying which (L13). After L29, the count of five matches my five homecomings.
- Ten pitched, this one kept. Dropped: the rooms belong to old owners (ghosts, banned tone), a hidden sibling (a sad secret), the rooms are for future people (time), the house grows on screen (a set move, the panel's note), Mom lives in them (no turn), the rooms are Dad's (no wait), the rooms hold my forgotten things (forgetting, crowded), a room for the dog (an animal), a locked room (a trap), the house shrinks when I leave (ep116's inches).
- Checked against "Claimed big ideas": not floor-thirteen (forgotten things on one floor), not moving-day (a house that grows to the tallest), not ep125 (a made-up room for a sleepwalker). Shape `extra-ones-are-me`.
- **The rule is the clue:** "a room for everyone who's ever lived in it" sounds like a count of people. After L29, it counts the people I've been.
