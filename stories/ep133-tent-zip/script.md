- **Batch 14 constraints, applied to every shot:**
  1. Flash-safe by design. The lantern is electric and steady, and there's no campfire, no flashlight beam swinging, and no lightning. Two slow light changes in the whole film: the moon coming out from cloud at L25 (1.5 s fade up of the blue fill) and dawn at L38 (2 s fade up of warm daylight).
  2. Two faces at most in any shot. Inside the tent it's me and the figure, never more. The Dev outside is only a voice until dawn (S39). Every emotional line is on a single close-up facing camera, with no profile two-shots: the camera sits at the tent's back wall, so we both face it, side by side. Nothing is held near a face.
  3. The scare is a face and a body's size, never a shadow on fabric or a small prop. The zipper is narrated and shown only as the door's red stripe, which needs no reading.
  4. No set moves. The tent never moves, sags or tips. Only the zipper slides, which is a small door.
  5. The rule marker lives in the scene: a fat red stripe painted across the tent's door flap, which lines up into one straight line when it's zipped, framed by the door in every tent shot.

## The big idea
**Once your tent is zipped at dark, you can't unzip it, because whatever's outside sounds like whoever you love. But the Dev begging to be let in was my cousin. The thing that zipped itself in with me at dusk, wearing his face, was the one the rule should have kept out.**

- **Why it clicks:** Dev narrates everything, but the Dev in the tent never says a word (L07, L10), he shakes his head slowly, like his neck was too long (L16, L17), the voice outside knows how the joke ends (L18 to L20), and the Dev inside never lies down, his sleeping bag rolled and dry (L22 to L24). After L31, every one of those is the thing.
- Ten pitched, this one kept. Dropped: the voice outside is the thing (the obvious reading, no twist), Dev is a ghost (death word, banned tone), I'm the thing (overused), the tent is a loop (ep84), the lake comes in (water flicker), a bear (an animal shape), a copy in a mirror (mirror-maze), the campground is empty (forgetting, crowded), Dev sleepwalks (ep125), the rule was made up (ep103's cover story).

## The creature (the full reveal)
**The thing in the tent:** Dev's face on a body too tall for the tent.
- **Wrong silhouette, in one line (playbook rule 1):** a seated man whose neck and torso run nearly twice too long, so his head lies sideways, flat against the tent roof, while his shoulders sit level with mine. It isn't a robot, a mascot, a snowman, an appliance, a box, a dummy or a costume. It's a person.
- **What's stretched (rule 2):** the neck (about 1.8 times the cast's) and the torso (about 1.6 times). The legs are folded and ordinary. The arms are long, with the hands flat on its knees at the end of the green sleeves.
- **What proves its size (rules 3 and 13):** the tent roof its head is bent against, and my head next to its shoulder in the same frame. The camera is at our chest height at the back wall, looking slightly up.
- **Its one lit identity cue (rule 12):** Dev's orange beanie, lit by the lantern. The face is Dev's drawn face, `neutral`, its eyes two small glints, the brightest thing in the frame (rule 7). It has no mouth line in the reveal (`mouth: none`, rule 8). Before L28 its face is always half in the dark side of the frame, so the missing mouth isn't seen.
- **Light (rule 5):** from below, by the lantern on the floor, warm-cream on the underside of its chin and beanie. From behind, the moon through the fabric gives a hard blue rim on the bent head and long neck. No light from the camera's side.
- **Materials (rule 9):** the green puffer vest and yellow hoodie, darkened by the shadow. No friendly pale colours on the long neck: it's in shadow, rim-lit only.
- **Continuity (it's never seen moving):** it sits on the rolled sleeping bag at my left in every tent shot from S09 to S37, always seated, hands on its knees. Before L25 (S09 to S24) it's framed from my side with its head in the dark upper corner, cropped by the frame's top edge, so only the shoulders, the vest, the beanie's edge and half its face show. The L16 head-shake is a cut between two head angles, never an animation. At S27 to S30 the moon comes out and the whole figure is seen, still. At S38 (dawn) it isn't there: the rolled bag is empty.
