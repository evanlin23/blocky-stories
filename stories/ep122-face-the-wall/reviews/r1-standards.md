## Must-fix
1. **L54, L55 (originality, the cadence):** "The rule says SOMETHING sits on your bed. / It never says WHO." is the "the rule never says ___" loophole line that rule-three (uploaded 11-04) and ep54-dance-hall (queued) both already say. Keep the logic and change the words. For example, L54 "The rule says / SOMETHING sits on your bed." L55 "Something. / Could be ANYBODY." Move the quotable-line mark with it.
2. **S34, S35 (batch constraint 3, the scare has to read as a face):** a gold rim and two eye glints on a dark front will read as a shape on a phone. Light it so the drawn eyes, nose line and mouth show under the cool moon fill (the face's features visible, still unidentified), with the curls rim-lit. The cardigan stays out of frame. ep128's stair figure uses the same rim-and-glints recipe, so that one changes (see its review). This one keeps its own.
