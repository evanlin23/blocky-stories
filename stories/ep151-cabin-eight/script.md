## The big idea
**Every cabin's rule card says never answer the guest in Cabin Eight, and all weekend nobody answers us, because there are only seven cabins on the trail map: our door is the eighth, we're the guest the rule is about, and checkout was two days ago.**

- **Wait, what:** the scary neighbor the card warns about is us. Every cold shoulder (no wave back, the clerk looking through Mom, the curtain at Cabin Six) was a guest obeying rule seven.
- Ten pitched, this one kept. Dropped: Cabin Eight holds a creature (creature), the eighth guest is a Keeper (canon as twist), we're ghosts (death read, kept out: nothing says we're gone, only that nobody answers and the road brings us back), the rules are a game (no chill), the card lies (no logic), Cabin Eight is the store (flat), the rule protects the jays (silly), Mom wrote the rules (ep126's small author), the guest is me from later (crowded), the cabins rotate (confusing).
- Checked against "Claimed big ideas": not ep86 or ep125 (the intruder was me, sleepwalking: here nobody is an intruder, we're just the ones nobody answers), not ep134 (extra rooms are me), not ep115 (a road that loops a car: here the road is a beat, not the idea). The idea is the rule turned around.
- **The rule is the clue:** "never answer the guest in Cabin Eight" is on our card too. We read it as a warning about someone else.

## World

- The eighth door sits at the path's far end, built just like the others. Nobody explains it.
