## The big idea
**All three doors open back into the room you're standing in, because you've picked one a thousand times already, and the only right door is number four, the one behind you that you came in by.**

- Ten pitched, this one kept. Dropped: one door is your own house (too gentle), a door that knocks back (knock-back), a door that's locked from your side (every-lock), the doors are a test the viewer always fails (no turn), the right door is the one you don't open (a riddle, no picture), behind one door is you from yesterday (time), each door is a life you didn't live (heavy), the doors are the same door turned (a puzzle, no feeling), the comment you leave picks for you (a gimmick), the door number is how many people went in (a count, ep62's lane).
- Checked against "Claimed big ideas" and every twist: not a reflection, not a swap, not a count of people. It's a loop you're already inside, with a way home behind you.
- **The rule is the clue:** "you live behind it forever" first reads as a threat about tomorrow. After the reveal, you already do: the room behind every door is this room. The last line ("Everybody DOES.") runs back into L01, so the viewer is about to pick again.

## The story
- **Premise:** A would-you-rather in a white room. Three numbered doors, one clue on each. Pick wrong and you live behind it forever. Comment your number before the countdown ends.
- **Rule / hook (L01, about 2.3 s):** "If you pick the wrong DOOR, / you live behind it / FOREVER." L02 tightens it to a game: "Three of THEM. / One clue EACH. / Comment your NUMBER."
- **The relatable want:** to win, and to be right in the comments.
- **The marker (the rule meter), on screen by frame 1 and in every shot:** the doors' big numerals 1, 2, 3, and a round lime countdown ring in the top-left corner, our own design, that fills during L03 to L08 and ticks down 3, 2, 1 at L09. At L16 a fourth numeral, 4, fades into the ring.
- **The example montage:** the three clues, one shot each (L03 to L07): a warm door that smells of your mom's cooking, a door your phone is ringing behind while your phone is in your pocket, a mat worn from feet going in and none coming out.
- **A concrete mystery on screen by 15 s:** your phone ringing behind door two while it's in your pocket (L04, L05, by 9 s).
- **How the viewer is the exception:** everyone else in this room picked a door. You're the one being told.
- **At stake:** you, and home (the hall light someone left on).
- **First real scare:** L11, L12 (16 to 18 s): door one swings open on this same white room, with you standing in it from behind, facing three doors.
- **The climax turns the rule's own logic:** "the wrong door" means any door in front of you. The rule never said the right door was one of the three (L15 to L17).
- **Twist and reveal (L11 to L17):** said plainly. "All three / open into the same ROOM. / THIS one." Then the way out: "It's coming from / door FOUR. / The one BEHIND you."
- **How the last 15 seconds turn over the first line:** door four is open with the hall light on for you, and then the sting: you'll turn back to the three, everybody does (L20, L21), which loops straight into L01.
- **The plants a rewatch catches:**
  1. From frame 1, a soft warm wedge of light lies across the white floor from behind the camera, the shape of an open doorway. Paid off at L15.
  2. Door two's round brass knob reflects a fourth door behind the camera, with a small 4 on it. Visible in S01 and S04.
  3. L06, L07: the worn mat is the mat you're standing on.
- **Ending shape:** an open way home, and a cold turn back. Loop.
- **Warmth:** home, in one picture: a warm hall with a lamp on beyond door four.
- **Sad family reveal?** No.

## World
One place: a bright, clean, square white room with a pale grey floor and soft even light. Three tall doors side by side in the far wall, painted lime `#8cff3a` (1), teal `#2fd1c4` (2) and orange `#ff8a2b` (3), each with a big white numeral and a round brass knob. A worn sisal mat in front of the three. Behind the camera, the way you came in: an open white door with a small 4 on it, beyond it a warm hall with a cream runner, a lamp with a mustard shade on, and a coat hook.
- **Behind each door (L11, L12):** the same room exactly, with you standing on the mat, back to camera, facing three doors.
- **No text anywhere.** Numerals only.
- **What's true underneath (never said):** nobody keeps this room. It's just where you end up if you keep picking what's in front of you.
