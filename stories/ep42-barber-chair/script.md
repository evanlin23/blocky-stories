# ep42-barber-chair

**Slug:** `ep42-barber-chair` (pitch #26, batch 4)

## Premise
In this town everyone hums one note of their own, all day long, and a small coloured ring over every head ripples with it. I'm the one person with no note: my ring is clear and silent. My stepdad Len runs a three-chair barbershop on the high street. When he married Mum, seven years ago, he put in a third chair for me to learn on. I never sat in it. This winter his amber note is fading, a little dimmer every evening I walk past the shop. And each evening a headless customer under a striped cape, its face drawn large on its chest, sits one chair nearer the empty third chair.

## Rule
The world's rule: everyone hums one note of their own, all day long (L01). It's shown, never explained: rings over every head, the town's sound a big sustained chord. A note on its own goes thin (L07, Pat), so a note needs company. That's why Len's fades. Nobody in the film says so.

## Twist
My note was never missing. It was kept in the shop built for three, in the third chair Len kept for me and swept every night. When I sit in it, the note comes into me, my ring lights for the first time, and my note sits right on top of Len's, the note his was made to go with. The first line "everyone hums one note of their own" opens with me as the exception. The last line says I never was one: mine was waiting in the third chair.
- **Plant 1 (L16 to L17, S19 to S21):** passing the shop on Tuesday, the shop hums a chord of three notes with only two barbers inside. The sound mix plays three sustained tones (A2, E3, C#4). The third is my note, heard again at L35 and at L64.
- **Plant 2 (L23 and L25, S27 and S30):** Len sweeps under the third chair every night, though nobody has ever sat in it.
- Bonus plant (L07, S09): Pat's note goes thin when her flatmate moves out. A rewatch knows why Len's has been fading.

## World
A small town's high street in deep winter. Brick shopfronts with painted wooden fascias left blank (no lettering anywhere), frosted cobbles, iron lamp posts, a double-deck bus in plain cream and maroon with no route number or words. The barbershop: a narrow shop with one big front window and a glass door with a brass bell on a spring above it. A striped pole outside in oxblood and cream (no lettering). Inside, black and white checker tiles, pale sage-green wall tiles, a long mirror along the left wall over a wooden counter, three oxblood leather barber chairs with chrome bases and footplates in a row facing the mirror, 1.7 m apart (Len's nearest the window, Sid's in the middle, the third nearest the back). Along the right wall, three waiting chairs by the door and a coat stand. At the back, a white porcelain wash sink under a single brass lamp. Three rows of ceiling lamps (front, middle, back) on separate fades. Counter props: combs, a water spray, folded towels, and on the counter in front of Len's and Sid's chairs only, scissors and clippers lying flat (see Safety props). The counter in front of the third chair is bare, because nobody works there.

## The rule marker
A thin glowing ring a hand's width above every head (torus, about 0.28 m across, 1.5 cm thick), in that person's own colour. It ripples: a slow travelling wave around the ring's shape (0.6 to 1 Hz), never a brightness pulse. Brightness is steady per person, so it can never flash. A fading note is a ring that is dimmer and thinner (Len's goes from 1.5 cm to 0.5 cm thick and from full to about 25% emission over L12 to L41, changing only between scenes or as a fade of 1 s or more). My ring is clear glass: a refractive torus with a faint white rim and no emission and no ripple, until L56, when it fades up to violet `#b07cff` over 1.2 s and starts to ripple.

The creature has **no ring**. It carries none of the town's markers.

## The creature: the Face-Chest (never named on screen)
- One model, built once in `set.py` and posed per shot. Always seated. An ordinary R6 body under a full barber's cape in teal and cream stripes (teal `#2a8c8c`, cream `#efe6d2`), cape to the shins, dark brown boots showing below. **No head:** the cape's high round collar closes on nothing, and its open top is flat black inside.
- **The one wrong thing: its face is on its chest.** The cape front has a plain cream bib panel, and the face is drawn there, large, filling the torso width (about twice a normal face): two big round eyes with heavy black rims and small bright highlights, set wide, thin flat brows, a long flat mouth line. Scare variant (face swap, same model): the mouth is a tall dark opening onto black, with no teeth, no tongue, nothing inside.
- Its arms and hands stay under the cape. Never seen moving, only nearer. It never touches anyone and is never closer than 2.5 m to Len.
- Light it for fear: shop front lights off in its shots, a cool key from the side (street lamp through the blind), a warm rim from the sink lamp, the eye highlights the brightest thing. Test still first: the S39 scare (chest mouth open, chair turned toward Len), phone size.
