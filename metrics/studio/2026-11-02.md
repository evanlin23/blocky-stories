## What it shows
- **Every new Short gets a 1 to 4 hour test burst from the feed, then views fall about 95% within the hour.** ep20 went 361 views/hour → 21, and ep38 288 → 10. Only back-row kept going: about 32 hours, 22,291 views on day one.
- **The gate is the first second.** 61 to 65% chose to view our new Shorts against 82.6% for back-row. The feed stops pushing a Short that loses over a third at the swipe.
- **Then people leave early.** About 0:40 to 1:02 average viewed on 2:16 to 2:27 Shorts (27 to 46%), against back-row's 1:57 (85%).
- **Rule-first didn't fix it.** ep38 (61.3%) and knock-back (63.1%) both open on "In this town/farmhouse..." and did no better than ep20 (64.7%), which doesn't. back-row opens on a concrete, personal mystery with a number that doesn't add up: "The driver on my night bus counted 6. I counted 5."
- **back-row's opening was rewatched.** Retention stays above 100% for the first 15 s (167% at 0:00, 127% at 0:03, 103% at 0:10), and Studio flagged the intro as holding viewers "longer than usual". After that: 91% at 0:30, 84% halfway, 69% at the end, with the steepest stretch 1:16 to 1:40 (82 → 75%).
