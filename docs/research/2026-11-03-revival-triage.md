## The picks

### 1. ep32-sitter → "Never Paint A PERSON In Here 🖌️" (path b)
**Engine:** the made one (the old myth of a sculptor whose statue comes to life), not any specific work's plot.
**Line 1 (ban):** "Never paint a person in Kofi's class. Paint the town." **The rule:** anything the art class
paints shows up for real by Thursday: bare pine and pencil lines first, then the colour fills in. **The reveal:** I
broke the rule and painted the big brother I always wanted (Theo, 18 or older). Mom calmly sets him a place at
dinner, because eighteen years ago she broke it too: she painted me. **Plant 1:** the patch of bare pine behind my
ear that I've always called a birthmark (a close at about 12 s). **Plant 2:** Kofi says I hold a brush exactly like
someone he once taught (Mom, at her old easel). **Climax (the rule's own logic):** only a painted person's painter
can finish them, so I finish Theo's nose and Mom finally finishes my ear. **Reused:** the life room, easels, dais,
car park window, Mom's hall, the montage sets, the pine-and-guide-line face shader, Kofi and Mom, which is most of
the build. **New:** Theo, the ear-patch decal, a pine bench in the montage. **Cold check notes:** say "art class",
not "portrait class" (a portrait class that bans people reads as a contradiction). Show early that painted things
start as bare wood, so the ear lands at once. Give one line on why Mom left the ear ("I couldn't touch that canvas
again"). The narrator stays male (RULES), and nobody under 18 is in the cast. Watch the ban cap: ep80 is the queue's
other ban.

### 2. night-mower → "His Mower Makes Grass LONGER 🌿" (path b)
**Engine:** a man living backward in time (the old wizard-who-ages-backward myth). **Line 1 (whatif):** "What if
your neighbor's mower made the grass longer?" **The rule:** you never look at Odell's lawn while his 3 AM mower runs.
**The reveal:** Dad stays up to catch him and sees the clippings fly back up into the blades. Odell lives backward.
Our tomorrow is his yesterday, so he remembers our future, and in it he and Dad are best friends. **Plant 1:** in
S01, clippings fly *into* the mower (reversed, readable in one frame). **Plant 2:** Odell waves at Dad like he
misses him, and knows Dad's coffee order before Dad ever says it. **Climax:** Dad crosses the fence with two
coffees, to start the friendship Odell already remembers. **Reused:** S01 (Odell, eyes shut), the street, porch,
blinds, fence, alarm clock, mower and striped lawns, plus the opt-in `meter` overlay, which becomes a lime
grass-height ruler that rises while he mows. Most of the build is at commit 74d4ef6 on
`claude/batch6-p3-night-mower`. **Gone:** the Head and the eyelid, so the creature-4 blocker goes with them. A
creature is optional (RULES). The eerie comes from the reversed clippings and Odell's knowing. **New:** reversed
clipping particles, the coffee beat. **Cold check notes** (the first wording put the friendship in Dad's past, and the
fixed wording passed): say once that he mows with his eyes shut because he's already seen every pass, and that his
lawn looks "perfect" because it's the same every morning. Keep the porch and the floodlit lawn at the same
brightness (a warning now, not a block). This is not a sad family reveal.

### 3. ep43-reading-room → "Nobody Laughs In The Reading ROOM 🤫" (path b)
**Engine:** held-back feelings that take a body. **Line 1 (count, so the queue gets no second whatif):** "Everyone
here shows a number. My brother Tam's says FORTY." with the rule in line 2: "Days since you last laughed." **The
rule:** the glowing number over every head is days since your last laugh. Most people show 0 or 1. **The reveal:**
the Negative is made of every laugh anyone ever swallowed under the silent dome, and it drifts toward whoever is
holding in the most. Tam, cramming in silence, is at forty. **Plant 1:** a reader smothers a snort, and the Negative
is suddenly a table nearer. **Plant 2:** its mouth is an upside-down smile. **Climax (the rule's logic):** one laugh
resets you to zero. I break the silence with our dumbest old joke, Tam cracks, the whole room cracks, every number
rolls to zero, and the Negative comes apart into echoing laughter up the dome. **Reused:** the dome, the street, the
lamps and timer, Tam, the Negative model, the number rolls. **New:** the mouth decal, the joke, the laugh echo.
**Craft win:** nearly every crowd head shows 0 or 1, so only Tam's number and mine differ on screen, which removes
the number soup that sank r7. **Cold check notes:** say plainly what happens if it reaches him ("you'd never laugh
again"), show it break up at the laugh, and give Tam his reason in one picture (exam week). This is not a sad family
reveal.
