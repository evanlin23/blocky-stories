## How this was done (read first)

- **Watching:** each reel was played in full in Chrome. Burned-in captions were captured frame by frame (about every 0.05 s of video), and a thumbnail was taken at every shot change. Quotes are copied from the on-screen captions, so they match the spoken words as captioned. Where a caption was clearly mis-transcribed (e.g. "fucking dogs" for "barking dogs", "a long wet grape" for "scrape"), that is flagged rather than corrected. Short words (to, the, a) can occasionally be missing; those spots are marked with [brackets].
- **Audio was not available** (muted capture), so **voice pitch/number of voices, music and sound effects could not be assessed**. Voice *speed* is estimated from caption word rate. The number of speaking characters comes from the dialogue in the captions.
- **Cuts:** counted automatically from abrupt frame changes. Treat as ±15%.
- **Plays:** from the profile Reels grid on 2026-11-01. **Likes/comments:** from each reel's page on the same day.
- **Top 5 comments:** the 5 most-liked among the ~45–95 comments Instagram loads by default (the "top" sort). Copied word for word. One username containing a slur is redacted.
