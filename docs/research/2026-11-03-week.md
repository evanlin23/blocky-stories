## 3. Hypotheses for this batch
**H1. Long beats mid** (playbook H1). Long, 120 to 150 s: P1, P2, P5. Mid, 60 to 90 s: P3, P4, P6. P7 is the 20 to
45 s arm. Win: the long arm's median day-7 views at least 1.5x the mid arm's, without stayed-to-watch dropping more
than 5 points.

median comments per 1,000 views at least 0.6 across the batch (back-row: 0.31), and a comment quoting the marked
line on 3 of 7.

noun titles) can't run here: no two titles in the batch may start with the same word, so three "In This Town"
titles are out. The blind panel did point somewhere testable: its top picks were titles with an impossible count
("Five Riders, Six In The Mirror", "TWO Windows"). Count titles: P2, P7. Plain or warning noun titles: P1, P3, P4,
P5, P6. Measure chose-to-view (Studio) and day-7 views. Win: the count titles' chose-to-view at least 5 points over
the rest. A null result is likely and fine.

Each story session gets its slot (the table above) and follows this.

- Read first: CLAUDE.md, RULES.md (all of it, especially "The 2026-11-03 reset" and "Only good videos go up"), .claude/skills/week/SKILL.md, docs/research/playbook.md (the opening recipe, followed point by point), and docs/research/2026-11-03-week.md (this batch's brief: your pitch, its evidence and the other six). Run `git pull origin main` first (PR #117 adds the brief; if it hasn't merged, read it from branch claude/batch6-research).

How:
- Follow /week for one story ("/week 1"), skipping step 0.5 (the brief is done): writer, cold-viewer test, blind premise check, builder, critic and standards. Two review rounds at most, plus one rescue. If it's still average after that, drop it rather than ship it, and tell the lead why. The owner: "We shouldn't upload anything that isnt good... the bar should be very high."
- The opening recipe is mandatory: line 1 a warning to "you" (at most 12 words, 2.2 s), frame 1 looks straight at the warned-of thing already slightly wrong, a sound hit on its last word, line 2 tightens it, no names or backstory before 4 s, lines 3 to 5 show the rule at work one shot each.
- No sad family reveal ending. Narrator is male, US English, one marked quotable line, loop ending, "Comment WORD" pin, `"lane"` in package.json (core for town rules, experiment otherwise).
- The title may be improved, but it must not start with the first word of any other title in the batch: Our, This, He, Nobody, Every, Rule, Five (your own working title's first word is yours). Keep it about 5 words with one emoji at the end, no question.
- Look: use the polish pack (`look.polish: true`) if it has merged on main (a session tagged blocky-polish is adding it; `git pull` and grep blocky/ for "polish" before building, and again before the final). Use the CC0 assets and props in assets/ (kit.prop, kit.texture, look.hdri).
