# Branding: keep "PandaNanoAdventures" or rename? (2026-11-03)

The owner, 2026-11-03: "Do we have to fix our panda nano branding? With a rename new everything? We should download
and save the old stuff." And: "We can keep all of the old videos just privated." The owner makes the final call on the
name and applies it in Studio.

**Data.** `docs/research/brand-scan/2026-11-03.md` and `.json` (`scripts/brand_scan.py`, run on Actions, 1,265 API
units). It covers 51 fast-growing story channels: all 23 behind the 40 fastest blocky Shorts, plus 28 from the wide
scan that are new and at 100K+ views a day. For each it records the name, handle, description, banner and playlists.
It also ran 12 candidate names through YouTube's channel search. Avatars were classified by eye from a contact sheet
kept only as a 7-day run artifact. Names were ranked by a blind panel (one fresh agent, 19 names: our candidates
mixed with real channels and our current name).

Avatars (all 51):
- **30 show a single character face or figure** (18 of them blocky, mostly a head and shoulders on a bright flat
  background, often with a hat).
- 13 are a text logo.
- 9 are abstract or an object.
- 2 are a real person's photo.

What it means:
- **Short names win, and they don't need to say the genre.** 18 of the 23 blocky winners use one word (median 9
  letters), and only 3 of 23 name the genre. Ours is 19 letters. Confidence: medium.
- **The name isn't a reach lever.** The blind panel put five real hit channels (Gneef, Gelo Stories, PurpleHat, Ruckus
  Town, OutwardAnimates) at ranks 14 to 18 of 19, and they're winning anyway. 95% of back-row's views came from the
  Shorts feed, where the first second decides. Confidence: medium-high.
- **A character face is the standard avatar** (30 of 51). A text logo is the second choice and reads poorly at 98 px.
- **Descriptions set up a host or a world.** The two fastest new rule channels open their About in the voice of a
  recurring character who finds a strange town:
  - Ruckus Town, 9 days old with 128K subs: "I'm [host]. Every town I end up in has one weird rule…".
  - Gelo Stories: "[Gelo] just arrived in a strange little town…".
- **Playlists barely matter.** 42 of 51 have none.

## 2. Rename or not: **rename, once, now**
**Why rename:**
- **It's kid-coded.** The panel ranked "PandaNanoAdventures" last of 19 and flagged it "strongly" kids': "Panda",
  "Nano" and "Adventures" all read as a children's channel. Our art already draws young viewers, and hard rule 3
  (never made for kids) is easier to hold when the name doesn't point at kids.
- **It doesn't fit the stories.** A viewer who liked a creepy rule Short and taps the channel sees a name from
  another channel's life.

**What it costs** (YouTube Help, "Change, view, or hide your handle", checked 2026-11-03):
- The channel stays the same channel. Its ID, its 148 subscribers, every video and its view counts, and its
  monetization progress don't move.
- **The 94 old POV Shorts stay private on the channel**, as the owner said. A rename doesn't touch them.
- **The handle:** a new handle can be changed twice in 14 days. YouTube holds the old one for 14 days, then releases
  it, so `@PandaNanoAdventures` links stop working after that. Links by channel ID and by video keep working.
- **Search for the old name:** there's nothing public under it to find (all old content is private), so we lose
  nothing.
- The one real cost is that the 148 existing subscribers see a new name on their feed. That's fine, and a new face
  helps them notice.

**Why now:** before the first recipe batch and the Keeper pilot. If a 2026-11-09 Short breaks out, the name on it is
the one people remember. Then don't change it again.

**What not to do:** don't start a new channel. It would lose the 148 subscribers and the account's history for no
gain.

## 4. Avatar, banner, About and playlists
All art is rendered by our own engine (`brand/render_brand.py`, run with the bpy venv: `$BLOCKY_BPY
brand/render_brand.py both 48`). It has no letters and no outside assets.

**The Keeper of the Rulebook** is the avatar and the recurring host (`docs/research/port-study.md`): an adult night
clerk in a teal coat, mustard scarf and dark top hat, holding an oxblood rulebook with a brass hexagon glyph, with a

**About text** (paste into Studio → Customization → Basic info, swapping the name in):
> I keep the rulebook for a town that shouldn't need one. Every Short is one of its rules, and what happened to the
> person who broke it. Every story here is made up, and built and animated by us in blocky 3D. New rules most days.

It names the host and the world, and says it's fiction (hard rule 1). It doesn't say who it's for (hard rule 3) and
names no game (hard rule 4).

**Playlists:** most winners have none (42 of 51), so keep it to three, each made once it has 3 or more Shorts:
- "The Rulebook": the Keeper episodes, in order. Sequels are what viewers ask for.
- "Town rules": every creepy rule story.
- "Which one is wrong?": the horror puzzles.

## 5. Backup checklist, before anything changes (for the owner)
Don't delete anything. The old videos stay private on the channel.
1. **Name, handle and channel URL:** copy them, plus the channel ID URL (`youtube.com/channel/UC…`, under Studio →
   Settings → Advanced settings), into a note.
2. **Picture and banner:** Studio → Customization → Branding. Download the current picture and banner (open each,
   right-click, save). Also save the video watermark if there is one.
3. **Description and links:** Customization → Basic info. Copy the description, every link and its title, and the
   contact email.
4. **Layout:** Customization → Layout. Screenshot the featured video and sections.
5. **Playlists:** Content → Playlists. Screenshot or copy each title and its visibility.
6. **The 94 old POV Shorts:** Content → Shorts, filtered to Private. Note the count (94) and the 2 private long
   videos. Optionally save the list with Studio's export, or with Google Takeout → YouTube (videos and metadata) for an
   offline copy. They stay private either way.
7. **The community post** from March 2024 ("find panda nano new friends!"): screenshot it. Posts can't be made
   private, so decide whether to keep it or delete it once the backup exists.
8. **Channel keywords and default upload settings:** Settings → Channel and Upload defaults. Copy them.
9. **External places that link to the channel** (other socials, bios): list them, to update within the 14-day handle
   hold.
10. Then change things in this order: name, handle, picture, banner, About, keywords. Check the channel page on a
    phone and a TV.
