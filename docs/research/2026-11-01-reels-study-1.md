## Reel by reel

### DdewghptMrP: Soulmate Tracker (emori)
- **Premise:** everyone's wrist ring shows the miles to their soulmate. Toby's has always read 0.
- **Hook:** "A glowing digital ring floats above everyone's wrist, showing the exact distance in miles to their destined soulmate." On screen 0–3 s [S]: a smiling avatar on a sunny street outside a hospital. A cyan ring snaps onto his wrist at 0.75 s, and a push-in to "0 miles" in green comes at 2.25 s.
- **Beats:** 5 s Toby assumes zero means Clara. 15 s she leaves by bus and hands him a box: "Don't open this until I'm gone." 30 s "Instead, the number doesn't move." 32 s he opens it.
- **Twist:** the letter says "I made this device to make your ring believe we were." This flips the opening: "locked at zero" was faked all along. A flashback at 37 s shows Clara's own ring reading "200 miles" [S]. That's a visual plant the narration never mentions.
- **Lore/series:** the format is a "world rule" with an all-caps title in the description. The bald, suited protagonist avatar shows up again in the other two emori reels [S].
- **Ending:** a gut punch on the letter line, with no loop.
- **Look:** very close to Roblox [S]: smooth plastic, stock-style faces, hands that hold a box, bright daylight sets. The camera uses medium shots and inserts on the ring.
- **Captions** [S]: 2–3 words, lowercase white with a thin black outline, placed in the top fifth. No colour and no caps.
- **Voice:** the fastest of the four at 271 wpm, flat and storybook.
- **Music:** never drops out [S]. "What is the bgm?" suggests people notice it [I].
- **Comments:** outrage and confusion ("Genuinely what even was the point", "So did he love her or did he think they were soulmates"). It has the highest comment rate of the four, so I'd guess the argument drives comments [I].

## Across the sample

**What they do that we don't**
1. **The rule is a visible graphic from frame 0, and it keeps changing** [S]: "0 miles" → "200 miles", "+10$" → "$10 million", "SILENCE" → "BELONGING". You can follow it with the sound off. ep13's rule can't be seen.
2. **First line: "In this world/town, every…"** in 3 of 4 [S]. The rule applies to everyone, so viewers answer "what would mine be?" in the comments (Word, Sleep) [I].
3. **The twist arrives as a readable object:** a letter, a phone notification, postcards [S]. Viewers can pause and reread it [I].
4. **Brighter, closer-to-Roblox look:** daylight, smooth plastic, stock-like faces, characters holding things [S].
5. **Short, or long and emotional.** emori runs 36–50 s, and the top reel runs 2:34 but is heartwarming. Of the 4, 3 turn warm or bittersweet [S].
6. **The audio never stops:** zero silences [S].
7. **One unanswered planted detail** ("found something amazing") gets a question in the comments [S].
8. **Series signals** are a recurring format, a recurring lead avatar (emori) and a verbal tic ("You see", craftee) [S].

**What we do that they don't**
1. A real scare: a built creature, an eye-opening reveal, escalation by distance. Only Sleep has a scare, and it's a one-shot at the end [S].
2. Twists planted three times and shown, not explained. Word explains its own meaning ("as he finally understood") [S].
3. Colour-keyed caps captions and music drops before scares [S]. We also cut least often (0.38/s against 0.44–0.61) and look darker, which reads less like the game the audience comes for [I].

## Rules for our writer and builder

1. **The world rule is shown on screen by 1.0 s and changes at least 3 times.** The rule is a number, word or meter of our own design, floating on a character or object. Test it in `check`: a list of rule-graphic states with timestamps, the first at ≤1.0 s and at least 3 distinct values. *(Partly conflicts with hard rule 1, "keep the set free of text". Numbers and made-up words are allowed, but the owner should confirm that overlays count.)*
2. **The first line states a rule that covers everyone, in ≤16 words, using "everyone", "every" or "you".** Example: "In this town, everyone hears a knock the night before they move." **Conflicts** with "never reuse the 'At the PLACE, rule' first line; start mid-scare, on a sound, a question or dialogue."
3. **The twist is delivered by a readable object** (a note, a screen, a tag, a ledger), held on screen for at least 1.5 s, and that object is visible but unremarked in the first 25% of runtime. It fits "plant it twice", and the text must be made-up words (hard rule 1).
4. **Every story has both a scare and a warm turn**, and the critic writes down both timestamps, at least 20 s apart. This is already in "The story"; the change is making it testable.
5. **Leave exactly one planted detail unanswered on screen, and pin a question about it.** It fits the pin and canon rules, and the answer goes in `stories/lore.md`.
6. **Test a short cut: 45–60 s, in the same world as a full story, or alternate lengths.** Track likes per day for each. **Conflicts** with "2:15 to 2:30 long".
7. **Look: the first 3 s are lit bright** (key at least 2 stops over the ep13 level, no teal grade), skin is smooth plastic with no mottling, and dark sets come after the hook. **Conflicts** with "The look" (flicker, long shadows, fog), and possibly with note 21 ("consistent blocky characters"), since skin texture is part of the brand.
8. **Captions sit in the top fifth of the frame** (centre at 15–20% from the top), and every face stays below them. The size, font and colour keys stay. No conflict: RULES only says faces must stay out of the caption band. ep13 breaks that at 9 s and 105 s.
9. **Target 250–270 wpm measured, and cut at 0.5+ shots a second.** **Conflicts** with "about 290 wpm". The competitors measure 247–271, and ep13 measured 238 despite the 290 target.

(I'm not recommending a recurring lead avatar like emori's or a catchphrase like craftee's. Both clash with hard rule 5. The owner could still weigh one recurring sign-off line as a series signal.)
